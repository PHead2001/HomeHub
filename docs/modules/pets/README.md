# Pets and Care Logs

## Purpose and Scope

This module owns pet profiles, pet photos and species art, the living pet cards, pet detail pages, and feeding, medication, and general care histories. Medication countdowns also produce server-side reminders: when a medication's next due day arrives (household time zone), hourly upkeep writes a `pets` notification, using the same countdown rule as the UI; see [Notifications](../notifications/README.md).

## User-Facing Capabilities

- `/pets` lists pets as living cards (H09). Cards enter with the shared `Stagger` (at most 30 ms apart, 400 ms in all; instant under reduced motion). The page header holds the title and, for members who may edit profiles, the **Add Pet** action in the same row (as Shopping and Maintenance). With no pets, the shared `EmptyState` (`kind="pets"`) invites the member to add the first pet (the invitation and button only appear with `pets.editProfiles`).
- Each card shows:
  - the photo with a face-safe cover crop (`object-position: 50% 35%`, no face detection), or an illustrated species avatar when there is no photo, the photo is the legacy `placehold.co` placeholder, or the photo fails to load (D17);
  - a feeding ring that fills per feeding today ("1 of 2 today", green with "All fed today" when the plan is met; the count is a `NumberTicker`) and "Fed 2 h ago by Sam" from the latest feeding. "Today" is the household day (see **Household day** below). On an explicitly non-daily schedule without a structured count ("every other day", "free feed"), a day without a feeding reads "No feeding planned daily" instead of "Not fed today" (PE-18b);
  - one countdown chip per current medication ("Heartworm · Due in 3 days"; amber when due today or within three days, red when overdue), most urgent first (`currentMedications`: earliest due date, then name). Chip text is 12 px, 14 px on touch screens (read from across the room on the wall tablet). The card shows two; more fold into a "+N" chip whose tooltip and screen-reader text name the rest (never "+1": a third chip is shown instead). The detail summary shows up to four and the Medication tab shows all;
  - one-tap feeding (**Feed · 1.5 cups**) at the planned portion: the tap logs at once through the shared feed operation (see **Shared feed operation** below), and the toast confirms what was logged with **Undo** for 10 s, the shared Undo window (`UNDO_WINDOW_MS`, `src/lib/undo-window.ts`), for everyone who may log; its toast is in the pet's own group (`petFeedToastGroup`: `pet-feed:<petId>`, PE-8i, D-P13), so a newer feeding of the same pet replaces it while feeding another pet keeps this pet's Undo. If someone fed the pet in the last 90 minutes, or today's planned feedings are all logged, the tap asks first: "Feed Rex again?" with "Sam fed Rex 12 min ago." (Feed again / Cancel). The button's tooltip names the global shortcut ("Log a feeding from anywhere  F" on hover or keyboard focus, with `aria-keyshortcuts="F"`) through the shared `ShortcutHint` (`src/components/ui/shortcut-hint.tsx`, keys from `SHORTCUTS` in `src/lib/dashboard/shortcuts.ts`). Success plays `playFeedback('complete')`, or `'celebrate'` when the feeding completes the day (by the operation's committed count), shows a short paw-print burst (skipped under reduced motion) and a toast with **Undo** (10 s) for everyone who may log; a spinner shows while saving, and a failure toast offers **Retry**.
  - **hold to feed** (H09): press and hold the photo for 800 ms to log a feeding at the planned portion. A ring over the photo fills while holding (it appears after 150 ms, so a quick tap never flashes it), then paw prints burst from the photo. It goes through the same feed controller and operation as the Feed button (`useQuickFeed` → `useFeedAction` → `feedPet`, food type from the latest feeding, the same "Feed again?" check, toast, Undo and feedback), and only for members with `pets.addLogs`. Releasing early, pointer cancel (for example a scroll), moving off the photo and Escape cancel without writing; a held or cancelled press never opens the pet page, while a quick tap still does. One hold logs one feeding: holding past the end does nothing more, and a press while a feeding is saving is ignored. Mouse, pen and touch all work (the link under the photo is not draggable and has no touch callout). Under reduced motion the hold still works, with a still ring and no burst. The **Feed** button stays as the keyboard and assistive-technology alternative (one tap logs; the toast confirms what was logged and offers Undo) and is described by its pet's fed status (the status line's id) and then the hidden hint text ("Or press and hold the photo of Pixel to log 1 can.") via `aria-describedby` (PE-20b). Hold to feed is on the `/pets` cards; the pet detail page keeps the Feed button only.
  The pet name is the card link (stretched over the card; a long name without spaces wraps and clamps at two lines, PE-20d); the actions menu and feed button are separate targets above it (on touch screens the 36 px actions button grows to 48 × 48 through the shared `Button` sizing). Opening a pet morphs the card photo into the pet page hero (H12): on a plain click the card photo takes the shared name `pet-<id>-photo` (`petPhotoMorphName`, via `sharedId`) only for that navigation (so the theme reveal never captures it), and `useMorphNavigation` runs the View Transition; the hero on `/pets/[petId]`, its in-page loading skeleton and the route skeleton (`PetPhotoSkeleton`, from the route's `petId`) carry the same name. Modified clicks (new tab), clicks swallowed by hold to feed and every click under reduced motion are plain navigations. Hovering (mouse, `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) or keyboard-focusing the pet link for 400 ms shows the shared `PeekCard` (H26) with the type and usual portion, the schedule, the latest feeding and every current medication; it is `aria-hidden`, so the link name is unchanged. The home-screen shortcut **Log feeding** opens `/pets?feed=1`. This is the only feeding path with a confirm step: a launcher shortcut is not a tap on a pet, so it must not log silently. Once both the pet list and the member's permissions (auth and household membership) have loaded (`quickFeedDecision`), members with `pets.addLogs` get a **Log a feeding** dialog: straight to the confirm step ("Log 1 can of dry food for Pixel?" with "Fed 12 min ago by Sam · 1 of 2 today", **Log feeding** or Cancel) with one pet, a pet list first with several, each row showing that fed status (PE-19; "Checking last feeding…" while loading, "Last feeding unavailable" on a failed read). It logs through the same operation, toast and Undo; when the operation asks, "Sam fed Pixel 12 min ago. Feed again?" replaces the step in place (Feed again / Cancel), and the dialog stays open on a failure. Only after that decision is the param removed with `history.replaceState`; without the permission or without pets it is then ignored.
- `/pets/[petId]` shows the photo or species art (the morph target), the same feeding summary, medication chips and feed button, the schedule and usual portion, and tabs for Feeding, Medication and Care. `?tab=feeding|medication|care` selects a tab, also when it changes while the page is already open (a medication reminder tapped on that pet's page, PE-8f); choosing a tab writes `?tab=` back with `router.replace`. **Back to All Pets** has a back arrow (PE-18a). The pet's name wraps instead of being clipped. Each tab lists history first; the full form opens behind **Log with details**, **Log a dose** or **Log care**. History entries show who logged them (member avatar and name) and properly pluralized amounts ("1 cup", "1.5 cups"). An entry whose date is blank or unreadable shows "Unknown time" instead of breaking the tab (`readableLogDate`, PE-8b, PE-8c). A feeding shows when the pet ate (`date`, the effective time) and, when it tells something (backdated, saved offline with the device clock, corrected, or saved more than a minute apart), when it was recorded (`loggedAt`): "Saved offline with this device's clock. Recorded 9:10 PM." or "Time changed by Sam (was Nov 10, 9:10 PM). Recorded 9:10 PM." (PE-5e).
- The feeding form defaults to the planned portion and unit and the last food type, accepts any amount above 0 up to 50 (`step="any"`, so a parsed fraction such as "1/3 cup" → 0.33 is a valid default; the pet form and the parser use the same bounds via `isValidPortion`/`PORTION_MAX`), falls back to Dry when the last entry's food type is not a known value, and hides the date and time fields while "Use current time" is checked. Saving goes through the shared feed operation: with the current time (or a chosen time within a minute of now) it is a live feeding with the "Feed again?" check; a chosen earlier time is a backdated entry (`backdated: true`, `date` = the chosen time, no check); a time more than a minute ahead is refused inline ("Pick a time that has already passed."). The medication form has an optional **Next dose due** date that drives the countdown (its earliest allowed day is the household's today). Unit pickers show lower-case units ("cups", "cans"; `UNIT_LABELS`, PE-18e); stored values keep their case.
- **Change time** (clock button on a feeding in history, PE-5e): corrects a saved feeding's effective time, for example an offline entry whose device clock ran ahead, without deleting it. Its author may do this for 10 minutes after `loggedAt` (the delete window), members with `pets.deleteLogs` at any time. The dialog takes a date and time no more than a minute ahead of the device clock ("Pick a time that has already passed."), refuses while offline ("Connect to the internet…", so the feeding state stays right), and saves through `correctFeedTime` (a transaction: the log's `date` and `ampm`, `originalDate` on the first correction, `dateCorrectedAt`/`ByUid`/`ByName`; the state's count is recounted and, when this is the latest feed, `lastFedAt` follows). `loggedAt` never changes.
- Rules added with these dialogs never block editing an older pet: in the edit dialog a value the member did not change (the locked name, a long schedule, feedings per day, portion) is exempt from the length and range checks; a changed value must pass them. Legacy species values stay selectable. Saving the edit dialog with the type unchanged keeps the stored photo hint (`dataAiHint`, `speciesHintFor`), so a legacy "Other" pet drawn as a rabbit stays a rabbit (PE-8a); a changed type takes the new type's hint.
- The edit dialog resets on every opening, so a discarded draft or replacement photo never carries into the next save. Removing a photo in either dialog is a draft change; it does not submit the form (the shared uploader's untyped Remove button is wrapped so its click cannot submit).
- Add and edit dialogs share one form, each with **Cancel** besides the save button (PE-18d): photo, name, type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Small mammal, Reptile, Other; a legacy stored value stays selectable), food schedule, and optional feedings per day and usual portion (amount and unit). Field problems are shown inline under the field with `FieldError` (`aria-invalid`, focus moves to the first problem): missing or too long name, a name already used in the household (case-insensitive), missing schedule, feedings per day outside 1-12, portion outside (0, 50]. Toasts are only for outcomes; failures use `toastError`, so messages come from `friendlyError`.
- Deleting a pet or a log asks for confirmation; the pet alert names the pet. A feeding's delete button shows for members with `pets.deleteLogs`, and to its author for 10 minutes after logging (PE-2); deleting a feeding goes through `undoFeed`, so the feeding state stays right.
- Pet lists, pet documents and all three log histories are live (`onSnapshot`): changes by other members or devices appear without a refresh. Every Pets listener is a resilient listener (`petSnapshotListener` in `src/lib/pets/live-listener.ts`, on `resilientListener` from `src/lib/dashboard/resilient-listener.ts`, the HP-5 pattern, PE-8g), subscribed with `includeMetadataChanges` and counting only a server answer (`!metadata.fromCache`) as recovered, as the dashboard does, so a cached snapshot never resets the backoff or the permission-denied count and a member who lost access settles on "no access" instead of resubscribing forever: a failed read resubscribes with bounded backoff and at once on `online` or when the page becomes visible; while it is down a history (and the card's feeding status and next dose) shows its "unavailable" message, never an empty history: "Feeding history is unavailable right now. Reconnecting…", or after a refusal "…isn't available to you: your access to pets changed." (`unavailableText`). A pet list or pet page already on screen keeps its content and records the failure (`liveFailure`, `src/lib/pets/live-listener.ts`), with a notice beside it (`liveNotice` / `staleNoticeText`): "Reconnecting… changes by others will show again once Rex is back.", or after a refusal "Your access to pets changed. This is the last version of Rex that loaded." (no Try again); the next answer clears it. With nothing loaded the page says "Pets are unavailable right now" with **Try again** (resubscribes now). A refused read (`permission-denied`, after its retries) says the member's role does not include pets, without a Try again (PE-18c).
- Focus (PE-20e): after "Feed again?" closes and after a toast's Undo, focus returns to that pet's Feed button (`data-feed-button`); Edit and Delete opened from a pet's menu return focus to its actions button (`data-pet-actions`), or to the page heading (`data-pets-heading`) when the pet is gone; **Change time** returns to the history button that opened it. Hold to feed always has the Feed button as its tap alternative; the peek card is extra information only.
- The medication chips' amber states stay on a 10% tint (the strength the AA preset check in `tests/unit/theme-contrast.test.ts` covers in every preset, light and dark); "Due today" is told apart from "Due soon" by a stronger border, not a stronger fill (PE-20c).

Species avatars (`src/components/pets/species-avatar.tsx`) are inline SVG for dog, cat, bird, fish, small mammal, reptile and other (a paw print), drawn in the EmptyState style with token classes only (`fill-primary/10` backdrop, `fill-muted` shapes outlined in `currentColor`, `fill-foreground` eyes, one `primary` accent), so every preset recolors them. Eyes blink (140 ms every 7 s, staggered between cards), and the figure idles: a 24 s ambient loop that sways and bobs it over the still backdrop by 1.5 px and 1.5 degrees at the default intensity (staggered between cards). The blink depth and the sway amplitude scale with the preset's single `--ambient-intensity` token (via `--ambient-scale`, 1 at 6%), shared with the aurora, shell tint and weather, so turning it to 0 holds the pet still (guardrail G5). Both are CSS-only animations that pause while the tab is hidden (`html[data-visibility="hidden"]`) and are removed under both `prefers-reduced-motion` and `html[data-motion="reduce"]`. The art stays SVG/CSS rather than the spec's Lottie (approved deviation: no new dependencies). The type picks the art; for "Other" the photo hint (`dataAiHint`, e.g. "rabbit") is used. Motion lives in `src/components/pets/pets.module.css`: the ring fill is a 500 ms data-change transition and the paw burst is 900 ms of opacity/transform in an absolutely positioned, `aria-hidden`, pointer-transparent layer, so nothing shifts or blocks input.

## Architecture and Data Flow

The pet list subscribes to the household pet collection; each card subscribes to its pet's newest 20 feeding logs for the feeding status. The medication countdowns (card, detail summary and Medication tab) does not use a bounded history window: `useMedicationCountdownLogs` subscribes to every medication entry with a `nextDueDate` and to every entry from the oldest pending medication's latest dated dose onward (`pendingWindowStart`), so a monthly dose stays visible however many daily doses follow, and a later dose of the same medication still supersedes it. Both queries use single-field indexes.

Log dates exist in two stored shapes: Firestore timestamps (feedings written by this app) and ISO strings (medication and care entries, and legacy feedings). Firestore orders values of different types separately, so one `orderBy('date')` with a limit could hide every entry of one shape (for example, new timestamp feedings behind 20 legacy string feedings). Every history and countdown query therefore runs once per shape (`date >= <min timestamp>` and `date >= ''`, each ordered and limited the same way), and `newestFirst` merges them chronologically before applying the limit. No migration is needed; documents keep their stored shape. The countdown's "since" queries use the same pair (the string bound has a one-day margin for date-only strings). Timestamp dates on medication or care entries are converted to ISO strings on read. The detail route subscribes to one pet document and mounts a live client per log collection (newest 100 entries each). Photo uploads happen before the pet document is written with the resulting download URL.

The feeding plan comes from the structured `feedingsPerDay`, `portionAmount` and `portionUnit` fields when set, otherwise from parsing the free-text schedule ("1.5 cups twice daily" → 2 × 1.5 cups; "One can morning and evening" → 2 × 1 can; ".5 cups" → 0.5 cups, never a number read from inside another one; amounts are rounded to two decimals and ignored above 50). Explicitly non-daily or irregular schedules ("twice a week", "every other day", "every 3 days", "as needed", "free feed", "Mondays") get no daily target, so the ring never claims a daily goal the member did not enter; "every 8 hours" counts as 3 a day. A stored structured portion outside the accepted bounds is ignored for the plan, otherwise one cup with no daily target (the ring then fills on the first feeding); such a non-daily plan is flagged (`nonDaily`) so an unfed day is not shown as a missed meal.

**Household day (PE-7, 16.1 D-6).** Every day in Pets is the household's day (`household.timeZone`, default America/Chicago), through the shared `localDay` of `functions/src/shared/calendar.ts` (`petDay` / `householdDay`, the zone rule of `src/lib/household-day.ts`), never the viewer's device zone: the ring and "n of N today", "All fed today", the feed operation's count and "Feed again?" check, relative labels ("yesterday", "3 days ago"), medication "Due today" / "Overdue by 2 days" on the card, the pet page and the Medication tab (`currentMedications(logs, now, timeZone)`, counting household calendar days between the stored `nextDueDate` and today, as the server reminder does), the dashboard's pet facts (`computePetFacts` with the household zone) and the Calendar's medication days (`medicationEvents(entries, timeZone)`, `medicationDueEvents(entries, householdToday)`). A daylight-saving day counts once (23 or 25 hours). Relative time under 12 hours always reads in hours, so a feeding at 23:00 reads "1 h ago" at 00:30, not "yesterday" (PE-8h). All Pets consumers read one shared minute clock (`useNow` → `createMinuteClock`, PE-21c): one timer while anything listens, firing just after each minute boundary and at once when the page becomes visible, is restored from the back-forward cache or comes back online (a wake replaces the pending timer, so there is never more than one, and none after the last unsubscribe), so an open page rolls over at household midnight without a reload (PE-7d).

## Data Model and Persistence

- Pet: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}`.
- Feeding logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-logs/{logId}`.
- Feeding state: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-state/current` (see below).
- Medication logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/medication-logs/{logId}`.
- Care logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/care-logs/{logId}`.
- Photos: `households/{householdId}/pets/{uuid}.{extension}`.

Pet IDs are the slugified name (`slugify(name)`, no suffix). New feedings use the press's operation id as their document id (`feedLogId(opId)`, a random UUID, PE-23h); new medication and care entries use Firestore auto IDs; older entries keep their auto or numeric IDs. Feeding dates are written as Firestore timestamps (server time for live feedings, PE-5) and reads accept timestamp or ISO values; medication and care dates are ISO strings. A server time that is still pending in a local snapshot reads as its local estimate.

Additive optional fields (all absent on older documents, which read as before):

- Pet: `feedingsPerDay` (integer 1-12), `portionAmount` (number), `portionUnit` (`Cups`, `Cans`, `Scoops`, `Other`). Clearing them in the edit dialog removes the fields. `type` may now also be `Bird`, `Fish`, `Small mammal` or `Reptile`.
- Feeding, medication and care logs: `loggedByUid` and `loggedByName` (the member's first name at the time of logging: `loggerName` takes the profile's first name, else the first word of its display name, skipping any that is an email, else "Someone"; never part of the email). `scripts/ops/scrub-remaining-emails.mjs` rewrites older labels that are an email, in the logs and in the feeding state's `lastFedByName` (see [Household Governance](../household-governance/README.md), "Email copies").
- Feeding logs written by the shared feed operation: `loggedAt` (server time of the write, immutable), `repeat` (`none`; `confirmed` after "Feed again?"; `offline` when queued offline), `confirmOf` (with `confirmed`: the log id of the last feeding the member was asked about), `clockSource: 'device'` (offline only: `date` is that device's clock) and `backdated: true` (a chosen past time from the detailed form). After a time correction (PE-5e): `originalDate` (the effective time before the first correction), `dateCorrectedAt` (server time), `dateCorrectedByUid`, `dateCorrectedByName`.
- Medication logs: `nextDueDate` (`yyyy-MM-dd`, a household calendar date).

A pet without a photo is still written with the legacy placeholder URL `https://placehold.co/300x300.png` so older clients keep rendering it; current readers treat that URL and an empty value as "no photo".

### Shared feed operation (PE-23, canonical for PE-1; PE-5; PE-2)

Every feeding entry point (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` picker, the command palette's quick add `logFeeding`, the palette's Feeding mode buttons (through `useFeedAction`), and the feeding-log form, live and backdated) calls `feedPet` in `src/lib/pets/feed-operation.ts` (`runFeed` with the app's Firestore); there is no other feeding writer (`addFeedingLog` was removed and `pet-log-api.ts` refuses the `feeding-logs` kind). The dashboard's one-tap **Fed** (HP-33a: `PetFedButton` in `src/components/dashboard/one-tap.tsx`, on a hungry pet's house chip and Pets tile row) uses it too, through `useFeedAction` and `FeedAgainDialog`, with the pet's planned portion and the latest feeding's food type from the dashboard's loaded history.

`feeding-state/current` holds `lastLogId`, `lastFedAt` (timestamp), `lastFedByUid`, `lastFedByName`, `day` (household-zone `yyyy-MM-dd`, the day `countOnDay` counts), `countOnDay`, and `prev` (the same six fields before the latest feed, for Undo; null after an Undo or for the first feed).

- **One press, one feeding (PE-23h)**: the caller takes an operation id once per press (`newFeedOpId`; `useFeedAction.run`, or one per previewed feeding in the palette) and the log is created under that id only. "Feed again" and Retry (after an uncertain response or a failure) reuse it, so a retried or doubled run of the same press finds its first write (`replayed: true`, reported as saved, nothing written) instead of adding a second; two sessions running the same press at once both end with one log (the second commit fails the "log does not exist" precondition, or is refused by the rules against the newer documents; either way the press runs once more on a fresh read and finds its log; a refusal that is real is refused again and shown as a failure). A tap while a feed is saving is still ignored.
- **"Feed again?" bound to what was shown (PE-23g)**: the asking result carries the last feeding it named and today's count; "Feed again" sends them back (`feedConfirmOf`). The transaction accepts the answer only while both are unchanged (`confirmStillHolds`); if someone fed (or a feeding was removed) since, it writes nothing and asks again (`staleConfirm: true`: "Someone fed since you were asked. Kim fed Rex 1 min ago. Feed again?"). If the question no longer applies (the feed it named was undone), the feed is plain. The confirmed log stores `confirmOf`.
- **Online**: one transaction reads this press's log, the pet (its plan's feedings per day), the state and the state's latest log (a latest feed whose log was deleted offline is not a feed), and, on every attempt, the surviving feedings from the server since the household day's start or 90 minutes ago, whichever is earlier (`dayFeedings` from `feedReadStart`: both date shapes, at most 100 each, newest first), so a feeding just before household midnight (for example an offline feed that synced at 23:55) still counts as recent at 00:05 even when the state document is older. Today's count is counted from today's logs among them (`countOnHouseholdDay`; the minutes before midnight never add to it), never taken from the stored count or the caller's loaded logs, so a feed queued offline that has synced is counted (online, offline, online gives 3), and a feeding deleted offline or while "Feed again?" was open is not; any feed or Undo by someone else writes the state, so the transaction retries with a fresh read. Then `planFeed` (pure, `feed-plan.ts`) runs `feedConfirmDecision`: when the last known feed is under 90 minutes old (`FEED_REPEAT_WINDOW_MS`; a time in the future counts as recent) or today's count `>= perDay`, it returns `needsConfirm` with who fed and when, and nothing is written; the UI asks "Sam fed Rex 12 min ago. Feed again?" ("You fed…" for your own feed; "Rex has had all 2 feedings today." for a complete day) and calls again with the same operation id and the bound answer. Meal slots (P2) replace the reason inside `planFeed`; the binding, idempotency and writes stay as they are. A device-authorized server action (lane F, F4) can read the same documents with the Admin SDK and call the same `planFeed`. Otherwise it creates the log (`date` and `loggedAt` = `serverTimestamp()`, `loggedByUid`/`loggedByName` of the signed-in member, `repeat` `none` or `confirmed`) and sets the state in the same commit, so two people feeding at once cannot both pass the check.
- **Offline** (the browser reports no network; transactions cannot run): the log alone is queued in a batch under the press's id with `repeat: 'offline'`, `clockSource: 'device'` and `date` = this device's clock (a press already queued on this device is not queued again); the check only uses what the device has (its cached state and loaded logs, `planOfflineFeed`, with the same bound answer), and the toast says "Saved on this device. Couldn't check whether someone else fed Rex." only once the write is queued. The state is not written offline (a blind queued write could replace a newer feed by someone else); the next online feed or Undo updates it, and every reader combines the state with the logs it has (the newer wins). A transaction that fails online (for example a dropped connection) shows a failure toast with Retry; nothing is claimed as saved.
- **Backdated** (the detailed form with a chosen earlier time): `backdated: true`, `date` = the chosen time, no repeat check; it becomes the state's latest feed only when it is newer than every known feed, otherwise it only adds to the count of its own household day (a different day than `day` leaves the count alone).
- **Undo** (`undoFeed`, from the 10 s toast and from the history's delete): a transaction deletes the log; when `lastLogId` is that log it restores `prev` (only if `prev`'s log still exists, read in the same transaction; otherwise the latest feed is cleared and readers fall back to their loaded logs) and sets `countOnDay` to today's surviving feedings (read from the server in the transaction, without the deleted log; this also repairs a count left by an earlier offline delete); when a newer feed came since, only the count changes, and if the deleted log is the one `prev` points at, `prev` is cleared too (feed A, feed B, delete A, delete B never brings A back). It never writes an old whole-document snapshot over newer data. Offline only the log delete is queued; the next online feed or Undo recounts the day from the logs. Undoing the record does not undo the meal (audit 17.2, WT-20): the next feed still runs the last-fed check against the restored state.
- **No state yet** (pets fed before this release, until the backfill runs): readers treat it as "no recent feed known" and fall back to the newest logs they have loaded (`knownFromLogs`: the card's newest 20, or the palette's newest entry per date shape); the first online feed creates the state (its count comes from today's logs, as always online); the palette reads the newest 20 feedings per date shape, as the cards do.
- "Today" for the count is the household day (see **Household day** above). The day is estimated with this device's clock at commit, so a feed within seconds of midnight on a badly skewed device can land on the neighbouring day. A feeding up to 12 h in the future (`FEEDING_CLOCK_SKEW_MS`, a slow device clock reading server time) counts as just now instead of being hidden; further ahead it is ignored (and can be corrected with **Change time**).
- `FEED_UNDO_MS` is the shared Undo window (`UNDO_WINDOW_MS`, 10 s, `src/lib/undo-window.ts`).

Migration: `node scripts/ops/backfill-feeding-state.mjs --project <id>` (dry run, counts only; `--apply` creates; `--firebase-tools-login` as the other ops scripts) creates `feeding-state/current` for every pet that has feedings and no state, from its newest 20 feedings per date shape (`feedingStateFromLogs` in `scripts/ops/feeding-state.mjs`: the newest becomes the latest feed, `countOnDay` counts that log's household day). Create-only and resumable: an existing state is never touched, and one created by a client meanwhile wins. Run it after rules R1 are deployed.

## Authentication, Roles, and Security

Pages require an authenticated current user and scope all paths with that user's `householdId`. Firestore and Storage rules treat `households/{householdId}/members/{uid}` as the only membership authority: the caller needs an approved member record (role `owner`, `admin`, `member`, `child`, or `guest` with `status` `active`), and `households.memberEmails` or user-profile fields never grant access. Pending `newuser` members are blocked.

Firestore rules enforce each pet permission (role preset plus per-member overrides; owners hold every permission): `pets.view` to read pets and logs, `pets.editProfiles` to create, update, or delete pet documents, `pets.addLogs` to create or update feeding, medication, and care logs, and `pets.deleteLogs` to delete logs. The UI now mirrors these with the effective `permissions` from auth context: Add Pet, the empty-state action and the pet actions menu need `pets.editProfiles`; feed buttons and log forms need `pets.addLogs`; the feeding Undo toast shows to everyone who may log (`pets.addLogs`), a feeding's delete and **Change time** buttons show to members with `pets.deleteLogs` and to its author for 10 minutes after logging, and medication and care delete buttons need `pets.deleteLogs`. Hiding controls is a convenience only; the rules remain the enforcement point.

Log shapes (QA-8): feeding logs may only set `date` (timestamp or string), `cups` (number 0 to 1000), `foodType`, `foodAmountType`, `comments` (up to 2000), `ampm` (`AM`/`PM`), `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName` and the feed operation's and correction's fields (below); medication logs `date`, `medication`, `dosage` (up to 200 each), `notes` (up to 2000), `nextDueDate`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName`; care logs `date`, `activity` (up to 200), `notes`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName`. On create, `loggedByUid`, when present, must be the writer's uid and `loggedByName` is only accepted with it; both stay optional because clients cached from before 2026-11-06 write neither. On update both are immutable (no change, no removal). Medication and care entries may still be created without them (clients cached from before 2026-11-06); feedings may not since stage R2.

Feeding rules, stage R1 (shipped in 2.1, still in force): feeding logs also allow `loggedAt` (a timestamp equal to `request.time` on create, immutable afterwards), `repeat` (`none`/`confirmed`/`offline`), `clockSource` (`device`/`server`) and `backdated` (bool). A feeding may be deleted with `pets.deleteLogs`, or by its author (`loggedByUid`, with `pets.addLogs`) until 10 minutes after `loggedAt` (PE-2), so Undo works for members and children; older entries without `loggedAt` need `pets.deleteLogs`. `feeding-state/{id}` allows only `current`: read with `pets.view`; no delete; every write must have the six fields with valid types (`countOnDay` an integer 0-1000, `day` `yyyy-MM-dd` or null, `prev` null or the same six fields). A create, or an update that changes `lastLogId`, needs `pets.addLogs`, `lastFedByUid == request.auth.uid`, a log at `lastLogId` that exists after the write (`existsAfter`) and is signed by the writer, and `prev` equal to the replaced state's six fields exactly (null only on create, when there was no state), so a member cannot plant a fabricated "previous feeder" for a later Undo to restore. With `pets.addLogs` or `pets.deleteLogs` an update may also change only `day`/`countOnDay`; clear `prev` (with the count) when the log `prev` points at is gone after the write; or, when the current `lastLogId` log is gone after the write (Undo), restore exactly the stored `prev` while its log still exists, or clear the latest feed only when there is no surviving `prev` (null never discards a feed that still exists).

Feeding rules, stage R2 (this release, after the 2.1 window with R1 and the feeding-state backfill; PE-23f/g/h, PE-5e), `feedingCreateR2` and `feedingCorrected` in `firestore.rules`:

- Every feeding create is signed by the writer (`loggedByUid == request.auth.uid`, required) with `loggedAt == request.time`, carries no correction fields, and is one of: **live** (`repeat` `none`/`confirmed`, no `clockSource`, not backdated): `date == request.time`, paired with a `feeding-state/current` write in the same commit whose `lastLogId` is this log; **offline** (`repeat: 'offline'`, `clockSource: 'device'`, live or backdated): the device time, at most 12 hours ahead of the server (the clock-skew allowance), no state write; **backdated** (`backdated: true`, `repeat: 'none'`): the chosen time, at most the same allowance ahead, no repeat check.
- A live `repeat: 'none'` create is refused when the state's latest feed is under 90 minutes old and its log still exists, or when the state's count after this feed is above the pet's structured `feedingsPerDay` (a complete day). A `confirmed` create must name in `confirmOf` the state's latest feed, or a feeding at least as new by effective time (its `date >= lastFedAt`: an offline feed that synced after the state was written, or a feeding whose time was corrected to after the state's latest); a confirmation asked before someone else's newer feed names an older log and is refused. "Latest" means the same on both sides: the client's `planFeed` takes the newer of the state's latest feed and the surviving logs by effective time (`date`), never by `loggedAt`, so a correction that makes an older feeding the newest (A 09:00, B 09:02, A corrected to 09:04) is named by the client and accepted by the rule; the state keeps naming B until the next feed. Without a surviving latest feed nothing is bound.
- A create over an existing id is an update, and the only update of a feeding is a time correction, so a reused id is refused (one press, one feeding).
- The state's new latest feed must carry its log's time (`lastFedAt == log.date`), so a state cannot claim an older feed than its log and skip the next member's question.
- **Time correction** (PE-5e): only `date`, `ampm`, `originalDate` and `dateCorrected*` change; the new `date` is a timestamp at most 12 hours ahead; `originalDate` is the pre-correction `date` on the first correction and unchanged afterwards; `dateCorrectedAt == request.time` and `dateCorrectedByUid` is the writer; `loggedAt` and the author never change. Allowed to the author with `pets.addLogs` for 10 minutes after `loggedAt`, or with `pets.deleteLogs`. The state may follow a corrected latest feed (`lastFedAt`, `day`, `countOnDay` only, `lastFedAt` = that log's corrected `date`).
- Limits, stated: a plan read only from the free-text schedule is checked by the client transaction alone (rules see only the structured `feedingsPerDay`); the state's `day` and count are written by the client; a feed that synced from offline but is not in the state yet is checked by the client transaction only; a member can still mark a feed `offline` or `backdated` deliberately (an honest client never does so online). These rules protect against stale and buggy clients and replays, not a member determined to log twice, who can always answer "Feed again".
- Compatibility: 2.1 clients' plain feeds, backdated and offline entries pass; their "Feed again" confirmation (no `confirmOf`) is refused when a latest feed exists, and their writes over pre-operation shapes were already refused. Deploy these rules with the client that writes `confirmOf`; an open 2.1 tab gets "Couldn't log" with Retry on a confirmed repeat until it reloads. Rolling back R2 means redeploying the previous rules.

Pet Storage rules require `pets.view` to read and `pets.editProfiles` to upload, replace, or delete photos. Uploads must be 10 MB or smaller with content type `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/webp`, `image/gif`, `image/heic`, `image/heif`, or `image/avif`.

## Integrations and Background Processing

Firebase Storage holds pet photos. The hourly upkeep Cloud Function (`functions/src/reminders.ts` with `functions/src/shared/medication-reminders.ts`) reads pets and their medication logs and writes medication reminders (`sourceType` `pet_medication`, due today or overdue, resolved once a newer dose is logged, and reopened if that dose is deleted); its pushes show the pet's photo. No AI flow or external API is involved. Sound and haptics go through the shared opt-in `playFeedback`.

## Invariants and Failure Behavior

- Log entries can be created and deleted but not edited, except a feeding's effective time (**Change time**, PE-5e), which keeps the original in `originalDate` and never changes `loggedAt`.
- Feeding, medication, and care history deletions share a target-specific confirmation dialog with cancel and confirm paths.
- Deleting a pet document does not recursively delete nested logs or Storage photos.
- Replacing or removing a photo does not delete the previous Storage object.
- A missing pet shows an `EmptyState` with a link back to all pets; a load failure shows "unavailable" with **Try again** while the listener keeps retrying, and a refused read says the role does not include pets (no Try again). A crash inside Pets shows the Pets error boundary.
- A failed log subscription is never shown as an empty history. The card and the detail page share `PetCareSummary`: a feeding load error shows "Feeding status is unavailable right now" (no ring, no "No feedings logged yet"), and a medication load error shows "Next medication dose is unavailable right now" instead of hiding the chip. The feed button stays available, but with an unknown status it never claims "all fed for today". The Medication tab shows "Next dose is unavailable right now", and each history tab shows its own unavailable message. A unit test checks that every consumer of `usePetLogs` and `useMedicationCountdownLogs` reads `error`.
- Quick feedings (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` confirm) never log before the pet's feeding history has loaded (`quickFeedFood`): until then the Feed button and **Log feeding** are disabled (the confirm shows "Checking Pixel's last feeding…") and a hold does not start, so a fast tap can never record a default Dry entry for a pet that eats wet food. Once loaded they log the latest feeding's food type (Dry with no history). If the history cannot be read, feeding stays available as Dry and says so ("…of dry food logged (last feeding unavailable)" in the toast, and a note in the confirm); unavailable history is never treated as empty.
- H09 accessible alternative (owner decision, wave 4): the audit's acceptance asks hold to feed for "a tap-plus-confirm alternative". The owner reads the single reversible tap (one tap on **Feed** logs, Undo for 10 s) as that alternative, so the card's Feed button has no confirm step of its own; it asks only when the shared operation's last-fed check does (fed under 90 minutes ago, or today complete), and the `?feed=1` shortcut keeps its confirm (see above).
- Undo of a feeding deletes that entry only (and keeps the feeding state honest, see above); it is offered for 10 s to everyone who may log, and the rules accept it from the author for 10 minutes.
- Duplicate names are rejected in the add dialog against the loaded list (case-insensitive). The pet id is the slugified name, so two clients adding the same name at once write the same document and the later one replaces the earlier (known; renames and ids are P5).
- Reduced motion (system or in-app) shows end states instantly: no blink, idle sway, ring transition, paw burst, card stagger, count ticker or photo morph.
