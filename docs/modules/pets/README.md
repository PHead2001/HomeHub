# Pets and Care Logs

## Purpose and Scope

This module owns pet profiles, pet photos and species art, the living pet cards, pet detail pages, feeding and general care histories, and medication courses with their dose history. Medication courses also produce server-side reminders: when a course's next scheduled dose comes due (household time zone), hourly upkeep writes a `pets` notification computed from the same schedule model as the UI; see **Medication courses** below and [Notifications](../notifications/README.md).

## Architecture and Data Flow

The pet list subscribes to the household pet collection; each card subscribes to its pet's newest 30 feeding logs per date shape, merged (`PET_FEEDING_WINDOW`), for the feeding status and the seven-day strip (enough for a week of four meals a day; when the merged list is at the limit, days the strip cannot see read "not loaded", never "missed"). Each card also subscribes to the pet's `medication-courses` (`useMedicationCourses`) and its offline doses (`useOfflineDoses`). Legacy medication countdowns (card, detail summary and Medication tab) do not use a bounded history window: `useMedicationCountdownLogs` subscribes to every medication entry with a `nextDueDate` and to every entry from the oldest pending medication's latest dated dose onward (`pendingWindowStart`), so a monthly dose stays visible however many daily doses follow, and a later dose of the same medication still supersedes it. Both queries use single-field indexes.

Log dates exist in two stored shapes: Firestore timestamps (feedings written by this app) and ISO strings (medication and care entries, and legacy feedings). Firestore orders values of different types separately, so one `orderBy('date')` with a limit could hide every entry of one shape (for example, new timestamp feedings behind 20 legacy string feedings). Every history and countdown query therefore runs once per shape (`date >= <min timestamp>` and `date >= ''`, each ordered and limited the same way), and `newestFirst` merges them chronologically before applying the limit. No migration is needed; documents keep their stored shape. The countdown's "since" queries use the same pair (the string bound has a one-day margin for date-only strings). Timestamp dates on medication or care entries are converted to ISO strings on read. The detail route subscribes to one pet document and mounts a live client per log collection (newest 100 entries each). Photo uploads happen before the pet document is written with the resulting download URL.

The feeding plan comes from the structured `feedingsPerDay`, `portionAmount` and `portionUnit` fields when set, otherwise from parsing the free-text schedule ("1.5 cups twice daily" → 2 × 1.5 cups; "One can morning and evening" → 2 × 1 can; ".5 cups" → 0.5 cups, never a number read from inside another one; amounts are rounded to two decimals and ignored above 50). Explicitly non-daily or irregular schedules ("twice a week", "every other day", "every 3 days", "as needed", "free feed", "Mondays") get no daily target, so the ring never claims a daily goal the member did not enter; "every 8 hours" counts as 3 a day. A stored structured portion outside the accepted bounds is ignored for the plan, otherwise one cup with no daily target (the ring then fills on the first feeding); such a non-daily plan is flagged (`nonDaily`) so an unfed day is not shown as a missed meal.

**Household day (PE-7, 16.1 D-6).** Every day in Pets is the household's day (`household.timeZone`, default America/Chicago), through the shared `localDay` of `functions/src/shared/calendar.ts` (`petDay` / `householdDay`, the zone rule of `src/lib/household-day.ts`), never the viewer's device zone: the ring and "n of N today", "All fed today", the feed operation's count and "Feed again?" check, relative labels ("yesterday", "3 days ago"), medication "Due today" / "Overdue by 2 days" on the card, the pet page and the Medication tab (`currentMedications(logs, now, timeZone)`, counting household calendar days between the stored `nextDueDate` and today, as the server reminder does), the dashboard's pet facts (`computePetFacts` with the household zone) and the Calendar's medication days (`medicationEvents(entries, timeZone)`, `medicationDueEvents(entries, householdToday)`). A daylight-saving day counts once (23 or 25 hours). Relative time under 12 hours always reads in hours, so a feeding at 23:00 reads "1 h ago" at 00:30, not "yesterday" (PE-8h). All Pets consumers read one shared minute clock (`useNow` → `createMinuteClock`, PE-21c): one timer while anything listens, firing just after each minute boundary and at once when the page becomes visible, is restored from the back-forward cache or comes back online (a wake replaces the pending timer, so there is never more than one, and none after the last unsubscribe), so an open page rolls over at household midnight without a reload (PE-7d).

## Data Model and Persistence

- Pet: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}`.
- Feeding logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-logs/{logId}`.
- Feeding state: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-state/current` (see below).
- Medication logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/medication-logs/{logId}`.
- Medication courses: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/medication-courses/{courseId}` (P3).
- Care logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/care-logs/{logId}`.
- Photos: `households/{householdId}/pets/{uuid}.{extension}`.

Pet IDs are the slugified name (`slugify(name)`, no suffix). New feedings use the press's operation id as their document id (`feedLogId(opId)`, a random UUID, PE-23h); new medication and care entries use Firestore auto IDs; older entries keep their auto or numeric IDs. Feeding dates are written as Firestore timestamps (server time for live feedings, PE-5) and reads accept timestamp or ISO values; medication and care dates are ISO strings. A server time that is still pending in a local snapshot reads as its local estimate.

Additive optional fields (all absent on older documents, which read as before):

- Pet: `feedingsPerDay` (integer 1-12), `portionAmount` (number), `portionUnit` (`Cups`, `Cans`, `Scoops`, `Other`). Clearing them in the edit dialog removes the fields. `type` may now also be `Bird`, `Fish`, `Small mammal` or `Reptile`.
- Feeding, medication and care logs: `loggedByUid` and `loggedByName` (the member's first name at the time of logging: `loggerName` takes the profile's first name, else the first word of its display name, skipping any that is an email, else "Someone"; never part of the email). `scripts/ops/scrub-remaining-emails.mjs` rewrites older labels that are an email, in the logs and in the feeding state's `lastFedByName` (see [Household Governance](../household-governance/README.md), "Email copies").
- Pet: `mealPlan` and `mealPlanHistory` (see **Meal slots** above).
- Feeding logs written by the shared feed operation: `slotId` (live feeds of a pet with meal slots today: the slot it was for; never changed afterwards), `loggedAt` (server time of the write, immutable), `repeat` (`none`; `confirmed` after "Feed again?"; `offline` when queued offline), `confirmOf` (with `confirmed`: the log id of the last feeding the member was asked about), `clockSource: 'device'` (offline only: `date` is that device's clock) and `backdated: true` (a chosen past time from the detailed form). After a time correction (PE-5e): `originalDate` (the effective time before the first correction), `dateCorrectedAt` (server time), `dateCorrectedByUid`, `dateCorrectedByName`.
- Medication logs (legacy entries only): `nextDueDate` (`yyyy-MM-dd`, a household calendar date). Course doses and one-off doses have their own shapes (see **Medication courses**).

A pet without a photo is still written with the legacy placeholder URL `https://placehold.co/300x300.png` so older clients keep rendering it; current readers treat that URL and an empty value as "no photo".

### Meal slots (P2: PE-6, PE-10, PE-19b, PE-22, PE-25, PE-26, PE-31; D-P3, D-P5, D-P18, D-P21)

- **Model (PE-22a).** `pet.mealPlan = { slots, effectiveFrom, confirmedAt, confirmedByUid, confirmedByName }`: up to six slots, each `{ id, name, time, amount, unit, offDays? }` with a stable id (`slot-<random>`, kept when the meal is renamed or moved), a name (1-30 characters), a household-local `HH:mm` time (distinct per plan), a portion (above 0, at most 50) and unit, and optional weekdays it is skipped (0 = Sunday … 6; at most six). The plan applies from `effectiveFrom`, the household day it was confirmed. A stored plan that does not read back valid (`readMealPlan`) is no plan: the pet is treated as unconfirmed, never guessed.
- **History (PE-22g).** `pet.mealPlanHistory` (oldest first, at most 10) keeps every plan that applied before: an edit on a later day appends `{ until: <new effectiveFrom>, slots: <the replaced slots, exactly as stored> }`; a second edit on the same day replaces that day's plan without an entry (it never judged a finished day); a first plan appends the legacy target it replaces (`{ until, perDay, nonDaily? }`). `planForDay(pet, day)` returns the current plan from its `effectiveFrom` on, else the entry covering that day (each entry covers the days before its `until`), else the legacy target; days older than every kept entry use the oldest kept entry. So the seven-day strip and history judge each day by its own plan, never by today's edited target. Confirming also sets `feedingsPerDay` (the number of meals) and the usual portion (the first meal's), so older clients and the legacy rule stay consistent. `confirmMealPlan` computes all this; `updatePet` runs it in a transaction over the stored pet (online only), `createPet` writes a new pet's plan without history.
- **Time model.** Every comparison is in household-local minutes after midnight (`localMinutes` of the household zone), never instants: a slot at 02:30 on a spring-forward day counts as passed at 03:00, a fall-back day neither drops nor doubles a slot, and the day rolls over at household midnight (a dinner logged at 23:50 never fills the next breakfast).
- **Windows (D-P21, for D-P5).** A day's served slots, sorted, split the day: slot i's window opens 2 hours before its time (`SLOT_EARLY_MINUTES`), never before the midpoint with the previous slot (the first never before midnight), and lasts until the next slot's window opens (the last until midnight). A slot is `upcoming` before its window, `open` in it before its time, `due` in it once its time has passed, `missed` once its window closed unfed, and `fed` with a feeding attributed to it.
- **Assignment (D-P5).** A feed at time t is for the slot whose window is open if it is unfed; else the next unfed slot later today; when none remains from the open window on, it repeats the open slot. Missed earlier slots are never targeted. Feedings that name a served slot (`slotId`) feed it; feedings without one (older entries, offline and backdated ones, an older client) are attributed in time order by the same rule at their own time; a second feeding of a fed slot is a double feed on that slot; a feeding naming a slot not served that day (an off-day, a removed slot) is an extra of the day.
- **"Feed again?" (D-P5).** With slots today the transaction asks when the target slot is already fed (`complete` when every slot of today is fed: "Rex has had all 2 meals today."; else `slot-fed`: "Rex's dinner is already logged."), when no slot is served today (`off-day`: "No meals are planned for Rex today."), or when the last feed was under 90 minutes ago and the target's window is not open yet (`recent`: "Sam fed Rex 50 min ago. Dinner is at 6:00 pm."). Feeding a slot whose window is open never asks for recency alone (an early dinner after a late lunch). Without slots the 2.1 rule stays. The answer is bound to the state it was shown for as before, now including the target slot (`FeedConfirm.slotId`); a confirmed extra records the target slot, so the strip shows it as a double feed.
- **One shared status (PE-22h).** `mealStatus(pet, logs, now, timeZone)` is read by the card and pet page (`usePetCare`), the F picker (its rows and confirm step: "Dinner due 6:00 pm · 1 of 2 meals today", PE-19b), the palette's Feeding mode and typed quick add (`mealLineOf`), the dashboard's pet facts, one-tap Fed and Pets tile (`computePetFacts`: `needsMeal`, `mealLine`, `PetFeed.portionFromSlot`), the seven-day strip, the attention strip and the living-house bowl. `needsMeal` (a confirmed slot `due` now) is the only "needs food": bowl, amber, dashboard **Fed**, the brief's "next meal is due" and the attention strip's meal line. A pet without confirmed slots never needs food (D-P3; PE-31a-c: no estimated bowl, no hungry label from a guess), and a pet is not hungry all afternoon because dinner is still ahead (it is `upcoming`/`open`, not `due`).
- **Portions (PE-22d, PE-6e).** Every quick entry point (Feed, hold, F picker, Feeding mode, dashboard Fed, quick add without an amount) passes `portionFromSlot`: the transaction logs the assigned slot's portion (the client's computed one is only the fallback, offline or on an off-day, where the first meal's portion is used). The detailed form and a typed amount keep their own portion; their live feeds are still assigned to a slot.
- **Parser (PE-6).** `parseSchedule` reads clock times (24-hour and 12-hour), named parts of the day (synonyms merged: morning/breakfast/am, noon/lunch/midday, afternoon, evening/dinner/supper/night/pm, bedtime/late night; the most specific first) and counts ("once …", "twice", "2 meals", "3x", "every 8 hours"), part by part (split on commas, "and", "then" …). Each "once …" with its part counts once; a count that disagrees with the meals named, two different counts, more than six meals or an interval that does not divide the day is `ambiguous` (no suggestion); non-daily text is `nonDaily`; a stated time or part names the meal; a bare count gets default times flagged as guesses; per-meal portions are kept ("1 cup in the morning and 1/2 cup at night"). The legacy daily target uses its count (`parseFeedingsPerDay`; an ambiguous text keeps its stated count). Nothing it reads is saved until a member confirms it in the form.
- **Migration (PE-22i).** No silent conversion: every pet keeps its legacy count and portion until a member confirms meal times in the app. `node --import tsx scripts/ops/audit-meal-plans.mjs --project <id>` (`--firebase-tools-login` as the other ops scripts) is read only (it refuses `--apply`) and prints counts only: pets with a confirmed plan, a malformed plan, and for the rest what the confirmation will need (`suggest-times`, `suggest-parts`, `suggest-count`, `structured-count`, `count-conflict`, `ambiguous`, `non-daily`, `no-schedule`; `auditPetMealPlan`, the same parser the form uses).
- **Dashboard one-tap Fed (D-P22).** A pet with confirmed meal times gets **Fed** on the dashboard (house chip and Pets tile) only while a meal slot is due; a pet without keeps the earlier rule, **Fed** while today's legacy count is not met, in the neutral colour (never amber, never the bowl, never "waiting to be fed" or the brief's "next meal is due", D-P3), so households that have not confirmed meal times keep one-tap feeding (`oneTapFedVisible` in `src/lib/dashboard/facts.ts`).
- **Not built.** Meal-due reminders or pushes (D-P4); device-authorized feeds (F4: the plan and status are pure and take the documents a server action would read).

### Shared feed operation (PE-23, canonical for PE-1; PE-5; PE-2)

Every feeding entry point (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` picker, the command palette's quick add `logFeeding`, the palette's Feeding mode buttons (through `useFeedAction`), and the feeding-log form, live and backdated) calls `feedPet` in `src/lib/pets/feed-operation.ts` (`runFeed` with the app's Firestore); there is no other feeding writer (`addFeedingLog` was removed and `pet-log-api.ts` refuses the `feeding-logs` kind). The dashboard's one-tap **Fed** (HP-33a: `PetFedButton` in `src/components/dashboard/one-tap.tsx`, on a hungry pet's house chip and Pets tile row) uses it too, through `useFeedAction` and `FeedAgainDialog`, with the pet's planned portion and the latest feeding's food type from the dashboard's loaded history.

`feeding-state/current` holds `lastLogId`, `lastFedAt` (timestamp), `lastFedByUid`, `lastFedByName`, `day` (household-zone `yyyy-MM-dd`, the day `countOnDay` counts), `countOnDay`, `prev` (the previous surviving feeding: `lastLogId`, `lastFedAt`, `lastFedByUid`, `lastFedByName`; null when there is none), and, for pets with meal slots, `slotsFed` (slot id → the first surviving log of that slot on `day`), derived from the surviving logs like the rest.

**The state is derived from the surviving logs (one rule for every write).** The state is a cache of the logs, never a snapshot: every online writer (live and confirmed feeds, backdated entries, Undo and history deletes by the author or by `pets.deleteLogs`, and time corrections of the latest, the previous or any older feeding) reads today's feedings and the newest Timestamp-dated ones (`stateFeedings`) in its transaction and stores `deriveFeedingState` of the surviving logs after its own change: the latest and the previous feeding by effective time (`date`, never `loggedAt`), each at its log's stored time, author and name, and today's count. **One definition of "latest"**, shared by `deriveFeedingState`, `knownFromLogs` (so "Feed again?" and `planFeed`) and the rules: only feedings with a stored Timestamp `date` can be named (`isNameableFeedingDate`; the rules can only compare Timestamps, so a legacy ISO-string entry is counted for its day but never named by the state, never shown in "Feed again?" and never confirmed); among them the latest is the one with the latest stored date within the 12-hour clock-skew allowance (beyond it a date is ignored, as readers ignore it), and a live feed being written is the latest. The rules compare effective times with `feedingNow`: a date ahead of the server clock (a device clock running ahead, within the allowance) counts as now, so a live feed is never "older" than a feeding that synced from a fast clock (that feeding becomes the previous one) and supported skew never blocks a confirmed feed. So a corrected previous feed comes back at its corrected time when the latest is undone (feed A, feed B, correct A to yesterday, undo B: the state names A yesterday, and the next feed is not asked about it), and a deleted one never comes back. Offline writes queue the log only; the next online writer re-derives. `tests/unit/pets-feeding-invariant.test.ts` checks this over random sequences of every write.

- **One press, one feeding (PE-23h)**: the caller takes an operation id once per press (`newFeedOpId`; `useFeedAction.run`, or one per previewed feeding in the palette) and the log is created under that id only. "Feed again" and Retry (after an uncertain response or a failure) reuse it, so a retried or doubled run of the same press finds its first write (`replayed: true`, reported as saved, nothing written) instead of adding a second; two sessions running the same press at once both end with one log (the second commit fails the "log does not exist" precondition, or is refused by the rules against the newer documents; either way the press runs once more on a fresh read and finds its log; a refusal that is real is refused again and shown as a failure). A tap while a feed is saving is still ignored.
- **"Feed again?" bound to what was shown (PE-23g)**: the asking result carries the last feeding it named and today's count; "Feed again" sends them back (`feedConfirmOf`). The transaction accepts the answer only while both are unchanged (`confirmStillHolds`); if someone fed (or a feeding was removed) since, it writes nothing and asks again (`staleConfirm: true`: "Someone fed since you were asked. Kim fed Rex 1 min ago. Feed again?"). If the question no longer applies (the feed it named was undone), the feed is plain. The confirmed log stores `confirmOf`.
- **Online**: one transaction reads this press's log, the pet (its plan's feedings per day), the state and the state's latest log (a latest feed whose log was deleted offline is not a feed), and, on every attempt, the surviving feedings from the server since the household day's start or 90 minutes ago, whichever is earlier (`dayFeedings` from `feedReadStart`: both date shapes, at most 100 each, newest first), so a feeding just before household midnight (for example an offline feed that synced at 23:55) still counts as recent at 00:05 even when the state document is older. Today's count is counted from today's logs among them (`countOnHouseholdDay`; the minutes before midnight never add to it), never taken from the stored count or the caller's loaded logs, so a feed queued offline that has synced is counted (online, offline, online gives 3), and a feeding deleted offline or while "Feed again?" was open is not; any feed or Undo by someone else writes the state, so the transaction retries with a fresh read. Then `planFeed` (pure, `feed-plan.ts`) runs `feedConfirmDecision`: when the last known feed is under 90 minutes old (`FEED_REPEAT_WINDOW_MS`; a time in the future counts as recent) or today's count `>= perDay`, it returns `needsConfirm` with who fed and when, and nothing is written; the UI asks "Sam fed Rex 12 min ago. Feed again?" ("You fed…" for your own feed; "Rex has had all 2 feedings today." for a complete day) and calls again with the same operation id and the bound answer. Meal slots (P2) replace the reason inside `planFeed`; the binding, idempotency and writes stay as they are. A device-authorized server action (lane F, F4) can read the same documents with the Admin SDK and call the same `planFeed`. Otherwise it creates the log (`date` and `loggedAt` = `serverTimestamp()`, `loggedByUid`/`loggedByName` of the signed-in member, `repeat` `none` or `confirmed`) and stores the derived state in the same commit, so two people feeding at once cannot both pass the check.
- **Device clocks ("recent" on both sides)**: the rules judge "under 90 minutes" on server time; the device asks a little longer (90 + 5 minutes, `FEED_CLOCK_MARGIN_MS`), so a clock up to 5 minutes off never plans a plain feed the rules refuse. A clock further off can still plan one: when the rules refuse it, the press is run once more and asks "Feed again?" instead of planning the same plain feed (`serverRefusedPlain`), so it never ends in an endless Retry; the answer is written as a confirmed feed (an answer that still holds is written as confirmed even when the device clock no longer sees a repeat).
- **Offline** (the browser reports no network; transactions cannot run): the log alone is queued in a batch under the press's id with `repeat: 'offline'`, `clockSource: 'device'` and `date` = this device's clock (a press already queued on this device is not queued again); the check only uses what the device has (its cached state and loaded logs, `planOfflineFeed`, with the same bound answer), and the toast says "Saved on this device. Couldn't check whether someone else fed Rex." only once the write is queued. The state is not written offline (a blind queued write could replace a newer feed by someone else); the next online feed or Undo updates it, and every reader combines the state with the logs it has (the newer wins). A transaction that fails online (for example a dropped connection) shows a failure toast with Retry; nothing is claimed as saved.
- **Backdated** (the detailed form with a chosen earlier time): `backdated: true`, `date` = the chosen time, no repeat check; the derived state names it as latest or previous only where its time places it, and it counts on its own household day.
- **Undo** (`undoFeed`, from the 10 s toast and from the history's delete): a transaction deletes the log and stores the state derived from the surviving logs without it (`planFeedDelete`): the previous surviving feeding at its own current time becomes the latest, today's count is recounted (this also repairs an earlier offline delete), and a deleted feeding never comes back (feed A, feed B, delete A, delete B leaves nothing). Offline only the log delete is queued; the next online writer re-derives. Undoing the record does not undo the meal (audit 17.2, WT-20): the next feed still runs the last-fed check against the surviving feed.
- **No state yet** (pets fed before this release, until the backfill runs): readers treat it as "no recent feed known" and fall back to the newest logs they have loaded (`knownFromLogs`: the card's newest 20, or the palette's newest entry per date shape); the first online feed creates the state (its count comes from today's logs, as always online); the palette reads the newest 20 feedings per date shape, as the cards do.
- "Today" for the count is the household day (see **Household day** above). The day is estimated with this device's clock at commit, so a feed within seconds of midnight on a badly skewed device can land on the neighbouring day. A feeding up to 12 h in the future (`FEEDING_CLOCK_SKEW_MS`, a slow device clock reading server time) counts as just now instead of being hidden; further ahead it is ignored (and can be corrected with **Change time**).
- `FEED_UNDO_MS` is the shared Undo window (`UNDO_WINDOW_MS`, 10 s, `src/lib/undo-window.ts`).

Migration: `node scripts/ops/backfill-feeding-state.mjs --project <id>` (dry run, counts only; `--apply` creates; `--firebase-tools-login` as the other ops scripts) creates `feeding-state/current` for every pet that has feedings and no state, from its newest 20 feedings per date shape (`feedingStateFromLogs` in `scripts/ops/feeding-state.mjs`: the newest becomes the latest feed, `countOnDay` counts that log's household day). Create-only and resumable: an existing state is never touched, and one created by a client meanwhile wins. Run it after rules R1 are deployed.

### Medication courses (P3: PE-24, PE-27, PE-3, PE-4, D-P7, D-P8)

A **course** is a document with a stable id (a random UUID, or `legacyCourseId(key)` for a confirmed legacy medication; never its display name): `name`, `dosage` (both from the member, up to 80 characters), `schedule`, `startDay`, `endDay` (inclusive, optional), `status` (`active` / `finished`), `legacyKeys` (legacy names it took over), `lastDose` and `prevDose` (dose pointers: `logId`, `occurrence`, `occurrenceAt`, `givenAt`, `byUid`, `byName`), `coveredUntil` (set by a schedule edit, below), `createdAt`/`ByUid`/`ByName`, `updatedAt`/`ByUid`/`ByName`, `finishedAt`/`ByUid`/`ByName`. Renaming or correcting it keeps the id, so its doses stay attached and no phantom course is left (PE-24a, PE-24n).

- **Schedule** (the member's, never suggested by the app, PE-24o): `{ kind: 'days', everyDays: 1-90, times: ['08:00', '20:00'] }` (up to six household wall times, or none: due all day; every N days from the start day) or `{ kind: 'hours', everyHours: 2-72, firstTime: '08:00' }` (real hours from the first dose, so the wall time moves across a daylight-saving change) (PE-27a/b). Occurrences come from `occurrencesAfter` in `functions/src/shared/medication-schedule.ts`, shared with the reminders: key `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm` (or `yyyy-MM-dd`) on the household day, instant from `zonedInstant` (a wall time that happens twice is the first one, one that does not exist moves forward by the gap).
- **Next dose** (`nextDueOccurrence`, D-P7): the first scheduled occurrence after the one the latest dose covered (`lastDose.occurrenceAt`, or `coveredUntil` when later), within start and end. A late dose never becomes a new schedule, an early dose covers the occurrence it was given for, a backdated dose changes nothing (PE-24c, PE-24p). A dose has no next-due field, so nothing blank can end a course (PE-3, PE-24e). States: due on its household day ("Due 8:00 PM", "Due since 8:00 AM"), overdue after it ("Overdue by 1 day"), soon within three days, else upcoming. A missed dose stays the next dose until a dose covers it or a later one.
- **Give dose** (`GiveDoseDialog`, from a card chip, the Medication tab's **Give dose** and a reminder's `?dose=` link; PE-24b/c/d/i): prefilled from the course (dosage read-only from the plan, "Last given 2 h ago by Sam"), the occurrence it is for (`doseChoices`: the latest missed one when doses were missed, otherwise the scheduled time nearest to now; up to three missed ones, the latest given one and the next one can be picked), **Given** just now or earlier (within 7 days, never in the future), optional notes, and the next dose after it, read-only. One opening is one press (`newDoseOpId`): the log is created only under that id (`doseLogId`), so a double tap, Retry after an uncertain response or a second session running the same press records once (PE-24j). Recording an occurrence that is already covered (that one or a later one was given, or a schedule edit counted it) asks first, "Already given? Sam recorded the Today 8:00 AM dose 5 min ago. Record another dose anyway?", bound to the dose it named (`doseConfirmOf`): if someone recorded a dose since, it asks again naming them (PE-24k). The transaction (`runGiveDose`) reads this press's log, the course and whether its latest dose's log exists, runs `planDose`, then writes the log and, when the dose is at or after the latest one, the course's `lastDose` (with the replaced pointer as `prevDose`) in the same commit; two sessions giving the same occurrence serialize on the course document (one records, the other is asked). Success plays the complete feedback and shows a toast in the course's group (`petDoseToastGroup`) with **Undo** for 10 s.
- **Offline** (the browser reports no network; transactions cannot run; 2.1 let medication be logged offline): Give dose works for a course this device has loaded (the course and its doses come from the cache). The dose is queued create-only under the press's id (a press already queued is not queued again), marked `repeat: 'offline'` and `clockSource: 'device'` with `date` = this device's clock (or the chosen earlier time, `backdated: true`), and "already given?" is asked only from what the device knows; the toast says "Saved on this device. Couldn't check whether someone else already gave it." only once the write is queued. The course's `lastDose` is not written (a blind queued pointer could replace a newer dose by someone else). Every reader combines a course with its offline doses (`withOfflineDoses` → `offlineDose`, which counts in `coveredThrough`): the pet's chips and Medication tab (`useOfflineDoses`, a live query on `repeat == 'offline'`), the Calendar, the reminders (they read every log) and the next online Give dose (it reads the course's newest doses inside its attempt), so an offline dose covers its occurrence everywhere once it syncs, and recording that occurrence again online asks first, naming it. Removing a dose offline works for an offline dose, a one-off dose or a legacy entry (no pointer names them); a course dose recorded online needs a connection (its pointer must move). One-off doses are queued offline the same way. A queued dose the server later refuses (for example more than 8 days old when it syncs) is reported with a "didn't sync" toast. Course edits, Finish and the legacy actions need a connection.
- **Undo / delete a dose** (`runUndoDose`; the 10 s toast and the history's delete): deletes the log and, when the course names it, points `lastDose` back at `prevDose` (while its log exists) or at the latest surviving dose of the course (read inside the attempt: `courseId ==`, `occurrenceAt` descending, a composite index), else nothing; when a later dose by someone else is the latest it stays, and only a `prevDose` naming the removed dose is cleared (PE-24l). The copy says only the record is removed: "Only the record is removed. If the dose was given, it still counts." Reminders follow on the next upkeep run (a resolved reminder is reopened when its occurrence is due again).
- **Finish course** (`runFinishCourse`; Give dose and the course's menu; PE-24f/g): `status: 'finished'`. Its chips, Calendar occurrences and countdown end at once; hourly upkeep resolves its open reminder and creates no new one; its history stays. The toast offers **Undo** for 10 s (`runResumeCourse`, `pets.addLogs`). Finished courses are listed under "Finished courses" on the Medication tab and are never deleted from the client.
- **Create and edit** (`CourseFormDialog`, `runSaveCourse`; anyone with `pets.addLogs`, as anyone who logged a dose could set its next due date in 2.1): name (autocompleted from the pet's courses; a second active course with the same name is refused, PE-4c), dosage, schedule kind, times or interval, first and optional last day, with a preview of the next doses. The course id is created once per opening, so a retried save never makes two. Editing the schedule recomputes the next dose after the latest dose; when the new schedule's occurrence right after the given one is nearer to it than the previous occurrence and within half a dose interval, it replaces the given one (`coverageAfterEdit` → `coveredUntil`: 8:00 AM given, moved to 9:00 AM, so 9:00 AM is not due again; moving it to once daily at 9:00 PM keeps that evening dose due). No duplicate future occurrence (PE-24n).
- **One-off dose** (`runOneOffDose`): a dose outside any course (`oneOff: true`), kept in history, no schedule or reminder; idempotent per opening.
- **Legacy medication (D-P8)**: entries without a course (the 2.1 shape) keep working and keep their name-keyed countdown and reminders (`currentMedicationsOn`, `pendingMedications`), until a member acts on the Medication tab's confirmation card (`pendingLegacyGroups`, one per legacy name whose latest entry still has a next due date). When every entry with a next due date gave the same gap in days, that gap is offered as a suggestion ("every 30 days", starting on the pending due day, so the next dose stays the same); conflicting gaps or no usable gap ("genuinely ambiguous") get no suggestion. Actions: **Confirm schedule** (`pets.addLogs`; creates the course under `legacyCourseId(key)` with `legacyKeys: [key]`, so two members confirming at once make one course), **No more doses** (`pets.addLogs`; a finished course without a schedule takes the name over), **Merge** into an existing course (`pets.addLogs`; typos are merged by hand only). A name in any course's `legacyKeys` no longer counts down or reminds; its entries stay as history. Nothing is converted by a script: `node scripts/ops/report-legacy-medication.mjs --project <id> [--firebase-tools-login]` is a dry-run-only report (counts only; `--apply` is refused) of legacy names: taken over, ended, waiting with a suggestion, waiting and ambiguous.
- **History**: course doses show the course name as given, "(now <new name>)" after a rename, "Given <effective time>", the occurrence ("For the Today 8:00 AM dose", "(recorded again)"), and "Recorded <server time>" when backdated; legacy entries keep "Next dose <day>".
- **Permissions** (R5): `MEDICATION_ACTION_PERMISSIONS` in `src/lib/pets/medication-permissions.ts` is the one table the UI reads and any server action (the registered-tablet device gate, F4) reuses. Everything in a member's medication routine needs `pets.addLogs` (lead decision 2026-11-14, so 2.1 is not regressed: anyone who logged a dose could set its next due date, which is a schedule): give a dose, a one-off dose, create a course and edit its name, dosage or schedule, finish it and resume it, and confirm, merge or finish a legacy medication. Removing a dose needs `pets.addLogs` (own dose within 10 minutes) or `pets.deleteLogs`. Courses are never deleted from the client (a pet's delete cascade is server-side, D-P12). Children and guests follow their role's permissions (D-P19: children log, guests do not). On a registered tablet the device policy still makes course edits PIN-level (F4). The rules enforce the same.
- Device actions (F4) reuse `planDose` / `planUndoDose` and the transaction shapes with an Admin SDK transaction; none are built here.

## Authentication, Roles, and Security

Pages require an authenticated current user and scope all paths with that user's `householdId`. Firestore and Storage rules treat `households/{householdId}/members/{uid}` as the only membership authority: the caller needs an approved member record (role `owner`, `admin`, `member`, `child`, or `guest` with `status` `active`), and `households.memberEmails` or user-profile fields never grant access. Pending `newuser` members are blocked.

Firestore rules enforce each pet permission (role preset plus per-member overrides; owners hold every permission): `pets.view` to read pets and logs, `pets.editProfiles` to create, update, or delete pet documents, `pets.addLogs` to create or update feeding, medication, and care logs, and `pets.deleteLogs` to delete logs. The UI now mirrors these with the effective `permissions` from auth context: Add Pet, the empty-state action and the pet actions menu need `pets.editProfiles`; feed buttons and log forms need `pets.addLogs`; the feeding Undo toast shows to everyone who may log (`pets.addLogs`), a feeding's delete and **Change time** buttons show to members with `pets.deleteLogs` and to its author for 10 minutes after logging, medication buttons follow `MEDICATION_ACTION_PERMISSIONS` (see **Medication courses**: a dose's delete shows to members with `pets.deleteLogs` and to its author for 10 minutes), and care delete buttons need `pets.deleteLogs`. Hiding controls is a convenience only; the rules remain the enforcement point.

Log shapes (QA-8): feeding logs may only set `date` (timestamp or string), `cups` (number 0 to 1000), `foodType`, `foodAmountType`, `comments` (up to 2000), `ampm` (`AM`/`PM`), `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName` and the feed operation's and correction's fields (below); legacy (2.1-shape) medication logs `date`, `medication`, `dosage` (up to 200 each), `notes` (up to 2000), `nextDueDate`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName` (course and one-off doses: see the medication rules below); care logs `date`, `activity` (up to 200), `notes`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName`. On create, `loggedByUid`, when present, must be the writer's uid and `loggedByName` is only accepted with it; both stay optional because clients cached from before 2026-11-06 write neither. On update both are immutable (no change, no removal). Medication and care entries may still be created without them (clients cached from before 2026-11-06); feedings may not since stage R2.

Feeding rules, stage R1 (shipped in 2.1, still in force): feeding logs also allow `loggedAt` (a timestamp equal to `request.time` on create, immutable afterwards), `repeat` (`none`/`confirmed`/`offline`), `clockSource` (`device`/`server`) and `backdated` (bool). A feeding may be deleted with `pets.deleteLogs`, or by its author (`loggedByUid`, with `pets.addLogs`) until 10 minutes after `loggedAt` (PE-2), so Undo works for members and children; older entries without `loggedAt` need `pets.deleteLogs`. `feeding-state/{id}` allows only `current`: read with `pets.view`; no delete; written with `pets.addLogs` or `pets.deleteLogs`; every write must have the six fields with valid types (`countOnDay` an integer 0-1000, `day` `yyyy-MM-dd` or null, `prev` null or the same fields). Since this release the state rules check the derived state (`feedingStateHonest`, `feedingLatestMoveAllowed`) instead of exact snapshots: the latest feed and `prev` each name a log that exists after the write, at that log's own timestamp `date`, author and name (or the latest is all null); `prev` is another log and not newer than the latest by effective time (`feedingNow`: ahead of the server clock counts as now); and the latest feed may move back in time only when the feeding it named is gone after the write (Undo, delete) or its `date` changed in the write (a correction), otherwise it stays or moves forward (a newer or synced feed). The rules cannot query, so they cannot prove the client named the newest log; they guarantee the state names real feedings at their real times and never hides a surviving latest feed.

Feeding rules, stage R2 (this release, after the 2.1 window with R1 and the feeding-state backfill; PE-23f/g/h, PE-5e), `feedingCreateR2` and `feedingCorrected` in `firestore.rules`:

- Every feeding create is signed by the writer (`loggedByUid == request.auth.uid`, required) with `loggedAt == request.time`, carries no correction fields, and is one of: **live** (`repeat` `none`/`confirmed`, no `clockSource`, not backdated): `date == request.time`, paired with a `feeding-state/current` write in the same commit whose `lastLogId` is this log; **offline** (`repeat: 'offline'`, `clockSource: 'device'`, live or backdated): the device time, at most 12 hours ahead of the server (the clock-skew allowance), no state write; **backdated** (`backdated: true`, `repeat: 'none'`): the chosen time, at most the same allowance ahead, no repeat check.
- A live `repeat: 'none'` create without a slot is refused when the state's latest feed is under 90 minutes old and its log still exists, or when the state's count after this feed is above the pet's structured `feedingsPerDay` (a complete day). A `confirmed` create must name in `confirmOf` the state's latest feed, or a feeding at least as new by effective time (its `date >= lastFedAt`: an offline feed that synced after the state was written, or a feeding whose time was corrected to after the state's latest); a confirmation asked before someone else's newer feed names an older log and is refused. Both sides of these comparisons use `feedingNow` (a date ahead of the server clock counts as now), and `confirmOf` must name a Timestamp-dated feeding. "Latest" means the same on both sides: the client's `planFeed` takes the newer of the state's latest feed and the surviving logs by effective time (`date`), never by `loggedAt`, so a correction that makes an older feeding the newest (A 09:00, B 09:02, A corrected to 09:04) is named by the client and accepted by the rule; the correction itself re-derives the state, which then names A as the latest and B as the previous. Without a surviving latest feed nothing is bound.
- A create over an existing id is an update, and the only update of a feeding is a time correction, so a reused id is refused (one press, one feeding).
- The state's new latest feed must carry its log's time (`lastFedAt == log.date`), so a state cannot claim an older feed than its log and skip the next member's question.
- **Time correction** (PE-5e): only `date`, `ampm`, `originalDate` and `dateCorrected*` change; the new `date` is a timestamp at most 12 hours ahead; `originalDate` is the pre-correction `date` on the first correction and unchanged afterwards; `dateCorrectedAt == request.time` and `dateCorrectedByUid` is the writer; `loggedAt` and the author never change. Allowed to the author with `pets.addLogs` for 10 minutes after `loggedAt`, or with `pets.deleteLogs`. The state may follow a corrected latest feed (`lastFedAt`, `day`, `countOnDay` only, `lastFedAt` = that log's corrected `date`).
- Meal slots (P2): a feeding's `slotId` must name a slot of the pet's current plan (any create) and never changes. A live `repeat: 'none'` feeding with a slot replaces the 90-minute and complete-day checks: the state written with it must record this log for its slot in `slotsFed`, and the slot must not already be in the stored state's `slotsFed` for the same `day` with a log that still exists (`feedingSlotFree`). Only the client checks: whether the slot's window is open or the target is not yet due (household-local times), off-days, and feedings without a slot that are attributed by time; so with slots, a stale or buggy client can still feed a later, not-yet-open slot inside 90 minutes without a confirmation. `slotsFed` is part of the derived state (`deriveFeedingState`: every online feed, Undo, delete and correction stores it again from the surviving logs of `day`); the rules validate its shape (a map of at most 20) but cannot prove it complete, so, like `day` and the count, it is the client's derivation. It never names a feeding for the state's latest/previous checks (`feedingStateHonest` is unchanged). Pet documents: only `mealPlan` and `mealPlanHistory` are validated (shape and bounds as above, `confirmedAt == request.time`, `confirmedByUid` the writer, the plan cannot be removed, a later `effectiveFrom` appends exactly the replaced slots and keeps every earlier entry or drops only the oldest of ten, a same-day edit keeps the history, a first plan may record only the legacy entry ending on its `effectiveFrom`); `effectiveFrom` itself is the client's household day (the rules cannot compute it). Other pet fields stay as in 2.1.
- Limits, stated: a plan read only from the free-text schedule is checked by the client transaction alone (rules see only the structured `feedingsPerDay`); the state's `day` and count are written by the client; a feed that synced from offline but is not in the state yet is checked by the client transaction only; a member can still mark a feed `offline` or `backdated` deliberately (an honest client never does so online). These rules protect against stale and buggy clients and replays, not a member determined to log twice, who can always answer "Feed again".
- Compatibility: 2.1 clients' plain feeds, backdated and offline entries pass; their "Feed again" confirmation (no `confirmOf`) is refused when a latest feed exists, and their writes over pre-operation shapes were already refused. Deploy these rules with the client that writes `confirmOf`; an open 2.1 tab gets "Couldn't log" with Retry on a confirmed repeat until it reloads. Rolling back R2 means redeploying the previous rules.

Medication rules (P3, `firestore.rules`, "Medication courses and doses"): courses are read with `pets.view`; created by `pets.addLogs` (active with a valid schedule, or finished without a schedule naming legacy keys), always signed by the writer at server time with no dose pointer; never deleted. Updates are dispatched by the changed keys (keeping a dose commit within the rules' evaluation budget): the dose pointers (`courseUpdated` → `doseGiven`: the new `lastDose` names a log this writer creates in the same commit, with its occurrence, instant, given-at time and author, and `prevDose` is the replaced pointer or null; `doseRemoved`: the named log is gone after the write and the pointer names a surviving dose of this course or nothing; `dosePrevDropped`), finish (`pets.addLogs`, signed at server time) or its Undo / resume (`pets.addLogs`), or an edit (`pets.addLogs`: name, dosage, schedule, days, `coveredUntil`, `legacyKeys` only growing). Course doses (`courseDoseCreate`): the field allow-list, signed by the writer (`loggedByUid`, `loggedAt == request.time`), `date` = server time, or a chosen earlier time (`backdated: true`) within 7 days and never in the future, an occurrence key and instant (at most 92 days ahead) of an active course; when the course's surviving latest dose covers the occurrence, `repeat: 'confirmed'` with `confirmOf` naming that dose; a dose at or after the latest must move the pointer in the same commit. An offline course dose (`courseDoseOffline`): `repeat: 'offline'` with `clockSource: 'device'`, `date` at most 12 hours ahead of the server (the clock-skew allowance, as offline feedings) and at most 8 days old when it syncs, no `confirmOf`, no pointer write, its course existing (one finished while the dose was queued keeps it as history); a device-clock dose that does not say offline is refused. A create over an existing id is an update, and course or one-off doses are never updated, so a reused id is refused. One-off doses: `oneOff: true`, signed, server or chosen past time, or offline with `clockSource: 'device'` under the same allowance. Legacy 2.1-shape entries are still accepted (cached older clients) and may not carry the new fields. Every medication entry (course, offline, one-off or legacy) is created only while its pet document exists, so no dose lands after the pet's delete cascade (a queued offline dose is then refused). Medication logs and courses are readable by a registered tablet whose pets section is enabled (`sectionEnabled`, F1), and a legacy entry's update keeps any `deviceAttribution` (`deviceAttributionKept`, F2). Deleting a dose: `pets.deleteLogs`, or its author for 10 minutes after `loggedAt`, and a course dose only when the course's pointer no longer names it after the write. Limits, stated: the rules see only the latest dose, so a repeat of an older occurrence than the latest is asked by the client planner (and always needs `confirmed`), and they do not recompute the schedule (an occurrence key is checked for shape and range, the planner checks it against the schedule).

Pet Storage rules require `pets.view` to read and `pets.editProfiles` to upload, replace, or delete photos. Uploads must be 10 MB or smaller with content type `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/webp`, `image/gif`, `image/heic`, `image/heif`, or `image/avif`.

## Invariants and Failure Behavior

- Log entries can be created and deleted but not edited, except a feeding's effective time (**Change time**, PE-5e), which keeps the original in `originalDate` and never changes `loggedAt`. Medication courses are never deleted from the client; finishing keeps their history.
- A medication course's next dose comes only from its schedule and its latest dose (never from a gap between entries or an AI judgement, PE-24o); every dose names the course and the occurrence it covered; the client chips, the Medication tab, the Calendar and the reminders compute the same occurrence (`tests/unit/pets-medication-course.test.ts`, "client and server agree").
- Feeding, medication, and care history deletions share a target-specific confirmation dialog with cancel and confirm paths.
- Deleting a pet document does not recursively delete nested logs or Storage photos.
- Replacing or removing a photo does not delete the previous Storage object.
- A missing pet shows an `EmptyState` with a link back to all pets; a load failure shows "unavailable" with **Try again** while the listener keeps retrying, and a refused read says the role does not include pets (no Try again). A crash inside Pets shows the Pets error boundary.
- A failed log subscription is never shown as an empty history. The card and the detail page share `PetCareSummary`: a feeding load error shows "Feeding status is unavailable right now" (no ring, no "No feedings logged yet"), and a medication load error shows "Next medication dose is unavailable right now" instead of hiding the chip. The feed button stays available, but with an unknown status it never claims "all fed for today". The Medication tab shows "Next dose is unavailable right now", and each history tab shows its own unavailable message. A unit test checks that every consumer of `usePetLogs` and `useMedicationCountdownLogs` reads `error`.
- Quick feedings (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` confirm) never log before the pet's feeding history has loaded (`quickFeedFood`): until then the Feed button and **Log feeding** are disabled (the confirm shows "Checking Pixel's last feeding…") and a hold does not start, so a fast tap can never record a default Dry entry for a pet that eats wet food. Once loaded they log the latest feeding's food type (Dry with no history). If the history cannot be read, feeding stays available as Dry and says so ("…of dry food logged (last feeding unavailable)" in the toast, and a note in the confirm); unavailable history is never treated as empty.
- H09 accessible alternative (owner decision, wave 4): the audit's acceptance asks hold to feed for "a tap-plus-confirm alternative". The owner reads the single reversible tap (one tap on **Feed** logs, Undo for 10 s) as that alternative, so the card's Feed button has no confirm step of its own; it asks only when the shared operation's last-fed check does (fed under 90 minutes ago, or today complete), and the `?feed=1` shortcut keeps its confirm (see above).
- Undo of a feeding deletes that entry only (and keeps the feeding state honest, see above); it is offered for 10 s to everyone who may log, and the rules accept it from the author for 10 minutes.
- Duplicate names are rejected in the add dialog against the loaded list (case-insensitive). The pet id is the slugified name, so two clients adding the same name at once write the same document and the later one replaces the earlier (known; renames and ids are P5).
- Reduced motion (system or in-app) shows end states instantly: no blink, idle sway, ring transition, paw burst, card stagger, count ticker or photo morph.

- `deviceAttribution` (which wall tablet, which person, face or PIN; written by tablet actions, F4, docs/modules/devices/README.md) is outside every pet-log and feeding-state allow-list, so a personal client cannot add it, and updates of feeding, medication and care logs must leave it unchanged (`deviceAttributionKept`).
