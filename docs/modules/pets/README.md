# Pets and Care Logs

## Purpose and Scope

This module owns pet profiles, pet photos and species art, the living pet cards, pet detail pages, and feeding, medication, and general care histories. Medication countdowns also produce server-side reminders: when a medication's next due day arrives (household time zone), hourly upkeep writes a `pets` notification, using the same countdown rule as the UI; see [Notifications](../notifications/README.md).

## User-Facing Capabilities

- `/pets` lists pets as living cards (H09). Cards enter with the shared `Stagger` (at most 30 ms apart, 400 ms in all; instant under reduced motion). The page header holds the title and, for members who may edit profiles, the **Add Pet** action in the same row (as Shopping and Maintenance). With no pets, the shared `EmptyState` (`kind="pets"`) invites the member to add the first pet (the invitation and button only appear with `pets.editProfiles`).
- Each card shows:
  - the photo with a face-safe cover crop (`object-position: 50% 35%`, no face detection), or an illustrated species avatar when there is no photo, the photo is the legacy `placehold.co` placeholder, or the photo fails to load (D17);
  - a feeding ring that fills per feeding today ("1 of 2 today", green with "All fed today" when the plan is met; the count is a `NumberTicker`) and "Fed 2 h ago by Sam" from the latest feeding;
  - one countdown chip per current medication ("Heartworm · Due in 3 days"; amber when due today or within three days, red when overdue), most urgent first (`currentMedications`: earliest due date, then name). The card shows two; more fold into a "+N" chip whose tooltip and screen-reader text name the rest (never "+1": a third chip is shown instead). The detail summary shows up to four and the Medication tab shows all;
  - one-tap feeding (**Feed · 1.5 cups**) at the planned portion: the tap logs at once, and the toast confirms what was logged with **Undo** for 5 s (members with `pets.deleteLogs`). The button's tooltip names the global shortcut ("Log a feeding from anywhere  F" on hover or keyboard focus, with `aria-keyshortcuts="F"`) through the shared `ShortcutHint` (`src/components/ui/shortcut-hint.tsx`, keys from `SHORTCUTS` in `src/lib/dashboard/shortcuts.ts`). Success plays `playFeedback('complete')`, or `'celebrate'` when the feeding completes the day, shows a short paw-print burst (skipped under reduced motion) and a toast with **Undo** for members who may delete logs.
  - **hold to feed** (H09): press and hold the photo for 800 ms to log a feeding at the planned portion. A ring over the photo fills while holding (it appears after 150 ms, so a quick tap never flashes it), then paw prints burst from the photo. It goes through the same feed controller and writer as the Feed button (`useQuickFeed` → `addFeedingLog`, food type from the latest feeding, the same toast, Undo and feedback), and only for members with `pets.addLogs`. Releasing early, pointer cancel (for example a scroll), moving off the photo and Escape cancel without writing; a held or cancelled press never opens the pet page, while a quick tap still does. One hold logs one feeding: holding past the end does nothing more, and a press while a feeding is saving is ignored. Mouse, pen and touch all work (the link under the photo is not draggable and has no touch callout). Under reduced motion the hold still works, with a still ring and no burst. The **Feed** button stays as the keyboard and assistive-technology alternative (one tap logs; the toast confirms what was logged and offers Undo) and is described by hidden hint text ("Or press and hold the photo of Pixel to log 1 can.") via `aria-describedby`. Hold to feed is on the `/pets` cards; the pet detail page keeps the Feed button only.
  The pet name is the card link (stretched over the card); the actions menu and feed button are separate targets above it. Opening a pet morphs the card photo into the pet page hero (H12): on a plain click the card photo takes the shared name `pet-<id>-photo` (`petPhotoMorphName`, via `sharedId`) only for that navigation (so the theme reveal never captures it), and `useMorphNavigation` runs the View Transition; the hero on `/pets/[petId]`, its in-page loading skeleton and the route skeleton (`PetPhotoSkeleton`, from the route's `petId`) carry the same name. Modified clicks (new tab), clicks swallowed by hold to feed and every click under reduced motion are plain navigations. Hovering (mouse, `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) or keyboard-focusing the pet link for 400 ms shows the shared `PeekCard` (H26) with the type and usual portion, the schedule, the latest feeding and every current medication; it is `aria-hidden`, so the link name is unchanged. The home-screen shortcut **Log feeding** opens `/pets?feed=1`. This is the only feeding path with a confirm step: a launcher shortcut is not a tap on a pet, so it must not log silently. Once both the pet list and the member's permissions (auth and household membership) have loaded (`quickFeedDecision`), members with `pets.addLogs` get a **Log a feeding** dialog: straight to the confirm step ("Log 1 can of dry food for Pixel?", **Log feeding** or Cancel) with one pet, a pet list first with several. It logs through the same writer, toast and Undo. Only after that decision is the param removed with `history.replaceState`; without the permission or without pets it is then ignored.
- `/pets/[petId]` shows the photo or species art (the morph target), the same feeding summary, medication chips and feed button, the schedule and usual portion, and tabs for Feeding, Medication and Care. `?tab=feeding|medication|care` selects a tab. Each tab lists history first; the full form opens behind **Log with details**, **Log a dose** or **Log care**. History entries show who logged them (member avatar and name) and properly pluralized amounts ("1 cup", "1.5 cups").
- The feeding form defaults to the planned portion and unit and the last food type, accepts any amount above 0 up to 50 (`step="any"`, so a parsed fraction such as "1/3 cup" → 0.33 is a valid default; the pet form and the parser use the same bounds via `isValidPortion`/`PORTION_MAX`), falls back to Dry when the last entry's food type is not a known value, and hides the date and time fields while "Use current time" is checked. The medication form has an optional **Next dose due** date that drives the countdown.
- Rules added with these dialogs never block editing an older pet: in the edit dialog a value the member did not change (the locked name, a long schedule, feedings per day, portion) is exempt from the length and range checks; a changed value must pass them. Legacy species values stay selectable.
- The edit dialog resets on every opening, so a discarded draft or replacement photo never carries into the next save. Removing a photo in either dialog is a draft change; it does not submit the form (the shared uploader's untyped Remove button is wrapped so its click cannot submit).
- Add and edit dialogs share one form: photo, name, type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Small mammal, Reptile, Other; a legacy stored value stays selectable), food schedule, and optional feedings per day and usual portion (amount and unit). Field problems are shown inline under the field with `FieldError` (`aria-invalid`, focus moves to the first problem): missing or too long name, a name already used in the household (case-insensitive), missing schedule, feedings per day outside 1-12, portion outside (0, 50]. Toasts are only for outcomes; failures use `toastError`, so messages come from `friendlyError`.
- Deleting a pet or a log asks for confirmation; the pet alert names the pet.
- Pet lists, pet documents and all three log histories are live (`onSnapshot`): changes by other members or devices appear without a refresh.

Species avatars (`src/components/pets/species-avatar.tsx`) are inline SVG for dog, cat, bird, fish, small mammal, reptile and other (a paw print), drawn in the EmptyState style with token classes only (`fill-primary/10` backdrop, `fill-muted` shapes outlined in `currentColor`, `fill-foreground` eyes, one `primary` accent), so every preset recolors them. Eyes blink (140 ms every 7 s, staggered between cards), and the figure idles: a 24 s ambient loop that sways and bobs it over the still backdrop by at most 1.5 px and 1.5 degrees (staggered between cards). Both are CSS-only animations that pause while the tab is hidden (`html[data-visibility="hidden"]`) and are removed under both `prefers-reduced-motion` and `html[data-motion="reduce"]`. The art stays SVG/CSS rather than the spec's Lottie (approved deviation: no new dependencies). The type picks the art; for "Other" the photo hint (`dataAiHint`, e.g. "rabbit") is used. Motion lives in `src/components/pets/pets.module.css`: the ring fill is a 500 ms data-change transition and the paw burst is 900 ms of opacity/transform in an absolutely positioned, `aria-hidden`, pointer-transparent layer, so nothing shifts or blocks input.

## Architecture and Data Flow

The pet list subscribes to the household pet collection; each card subscribes to its pet's newest 20 feeding logs for the feeding status. The medication countdowns (card, detail summary and Medication tab) does not use a bounded history window: `useMedicationCountdownLogs` subscribes to every medication entry with a `nextDueDate` and to every entry from the oldest pending medication's latest dated dose onward (`pendingWindowStart`), so a monthly dose stays visible however many daily doses follow, and a later dose of the same medication still supersedes it. Both queries use single-field indexes.

Log dates exist in two stored shapes: Firestore timestamps (feedings written by this app) and ISO strings (medication and care entries, and legacy feedings). Firestore orders values of different types separately, so one `orderBy('date')` with a limit could hide every entry of one shape (for example, new timestamp feedings behind 20 legacy string feedings). Every history and countdown query therefore runs once per shape (`date >= <min timestamp>` and `date >= ''`, each ordered and limited the same way), and `newestFirst` merges them chronologically before applying the limit. No migration is needed; documents keep their stored shape. The countdown's "since" queries use the same pair (the string bound has a one-day margin for date-only strings). Timestamp dates on medication or care entries are converted to ISO strings on read. The detail route subscribes to one pet document and mounts a live client per log collection (newest 100 entries each). Photo uploads happen before the pet document is written with the resulting download URL.

The feeding plan comes from the structured `feedingsPerDay`, `portionAmount` and `portionUnit` fields when set, otherwise from parsing the free-text schedule ("1.5 cups twice daily" → 2 × 1.5 cups; "One can morning and evening" → 2 × 1 can; ".5 cups" → 0.5 cups, never a number read from inside another one; amounts are rounded to two decimals and ignored above 50). Explicitly non-daily or irregular schedules ("twice a week", "every other day", "every 3 days", "as needed", "free feed", "Mondays") get no daily target, so the ring never claims a daily goal the member did not enter; "every 8 hours" counts as 3 a day. A stored structured portion outside the accepted bounds is ignored for the plan, otherwise one cup with no daily target (the ring then fills on the first feeding). "Today" is the viewer's local calendar day.

## Data Model and Persistence

- Pet: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}`.
- Feeding logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-logs/{logId}`.
- Medication logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/medication-logs/{logId}`.
- Care logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/care-logs/{logId}`.
- Photos: `households/{householdId}/pets/{uuid}.{extension}`.

Pet IDs are the slugified name plus a random suffix. New log entries use Firestore auto IDs; older entries keep their numeric IDs. Feeding dates are written as Firestore timestamps and reads accept timestamp or ISO values; medication and care dates are ISO strings.

Additive optional fields (all absent on older documents, which read as before):

- Pet: `feedingsPerDay` (integer 1-12), `portionAmount` (number), `portionUnit` (`Cups`, `Cans`, `Scoops`, `Other`). Clearing them in the edit dialog removes the fields. `type` may now also be `Bird`, `Fish`, `Small mammal` or `Reptile`.
- Feeding, medication and care logs: `loggedByUid` and `loggedByName` (the member's first name at the time of logging).
- Medication logs: `nextDueDate` (`yyyy-MM-dd`, local calendar date).

A pet without a photo is still written with the legacy placeholder URL `https://placehold.co/300x300.png` so older clients keep rendering it; current readers treat that URL and an empty value as "no photo".

## Authentication, Roles, and Security

Pages require an authenticated current user and scope all paths with that user's `householdId`. Firestore and Storage rules treat `households/{householdId}/members/{uid}` as the only membership authority: the caller needs an approved member record (role `owner`, `admin`, `member`, `child`, or `guest` with `status` `active`), and `households.memberEmails` or user-profile fields never grant access. Pending `newuser` members are blocked.

Firestore rules enforce each pet permission (role preset plus per-member overrides; owners hold every permission): `pets.view` to read pets and logs, `pets.editProfiles` to create, update, or delete pet documents, `pets.addLogs` to create or update feeding, medication, and care logs, and `pets.deleteLogs` to delete logs. The UI now mirrors these with the effective `permissions` from auth context: Add Pet, the empty-state action and the pet actions menu need `pets.editProfiles`; feed buttons and log forms need `pets.addLogs`; log delete buttons and the feeding Undo need `pets.deleteLogs`. Hiding controls is a convenience only; the rules remain the enforcement point. No rules changed.

Pet Storage rules require `pets.view` to read and `pets.editProfiles` to upload, replace, or delete photos. Uploads must be 10 MB or smaller with content type `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/webp`, `image/gif`, `image/heic`, `image/heif`, or `image/avif`.

## Integrations and Background Processing

Firebase Storage holds pet photos. The hourly upkeep Cloud Function (`functions/src/reminders.ts` with `functions/src/shared/medication-reminders.ts`) reads pets and their medication logs and writes medication reminders (`sourceType` `pet_medication`, due today or overdue, resolved once a newer dose is logged, and reopened if that dose is deleted); its pushes show the pet's photo. No AI flow or external API is involved. Sound and haptics go through the shared opt-in `playFeedback`.

## Cross-Module Dependencies

The homepage AI Overview may include pet count and a seven-day care-log count for callers with `pets.view`. It does not infer health, neglect, or missed care and does not send raw pet histories to the model.

- [Identity and Profile](../identity-profile/README.md) and [Household Governance](../household-governance/README.md) supply user, household, role and effective permission context.
- [Firebase Platform](../firebase-platform/README.md) owns Firestore/Storage initialization and security rules.
- [Application Shell and Dashboard](../application-shell/README.md) reads pets and feedings read-only for the dashboard (both date shapes, judged with `feedingPlan`/`feedingStatus`, so "fed" there means today's target is met, as on the pet card) and logs quick-add feedings through `addFeedingLog` with the pet's usual portion unless an amount is given and the latest feeding's food type (`knownFoodType`, Dry without history), as the Feed button does (amounts parsed with `parsePortion`; gated by `pets.addLogs`). It links to `/pets/<id>?tab=feeding`. It also exposes pet navigation and the **Log feeding** home-screen shortcut (`/pets`), and provides the design foundation used here (`EmptyState`, `MemberAvatar`, `FieldError`, `toastError`/`friendlyError`, `playFeedback`, `useReducedMotion`, tokens, `glow-card`).
- [Notifications](../notifications/README.md) receives medication reminders (category `pets`) from hourly upkeep, deep-linking to `/pets/<petId>?tab=medication`. Changing the countdown rule in `src/lib/pets/care.ts` (`currentMedications`) must be mirrored in `functions/src/shared/medication-reminders.ts`.
- [Chores](../chores/README.md) owns the household calendar, which reads every medication entry read-only and shows logged doses plus upcoming doses computed with `currentMedications` (no separate rule).

## Invariants and Failure Behavior

- Log entries can be created and deleted but not edited.
- Feeding, medication, and care history deletions share a target-specific confirmation dialog with cancel and confirm paths.
- Deleting a pet document does not recursively delete nested logs or Storage photos.
- Replacing or removing a photo does not delete the previous Storage object.
- A missing pet shows an `EmptyState` with a link back to all pets; a load failure shows a friendly toast and a Try again action.
- A failed log subscription is never shown as an empty history. The card and the detail page share `PetCareSummary`: a feeding load error shows "Feeding status is unavailable right now" (no ring, no "No feedings logged yet"), and a medication load error shows "Next medication dose is unavailable right now" instead of hiding the chip. The feed button stays available, but with an unknown status it never claims "all fed for today". The Medication tab shows "Next dose is unavailable right now", and each history tab shows its own unavailable message. A unit test checks that every consumer of `usePetLogs` and `useMedicationCountdownLogs` reads `error`.
- Quick feedings (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` confirm) never log before the pet's feeding history has loaded (`quickFeedFood`): until then the Feed button and **Log feeding** are disabled (the confirm shows "Checking Pixel's last feeding…") and a hold does not start, so a fast tap can never record a default Dry entry for a pet that eats wet food. Once loaded they log the latest feeding's food type (Dry with no history). If the history cannot be read, feeding stays available as Dry and says so ("…of dry food logged (last feeding unavailable)" in the toast, and a note in the confirm); unavailable history is never treated as empty.
- H09 accessible alternative (owner decision, wave 4): the audit's acceptance asks hold to feed for "a tap-plus-confirm alternative". The owner reads the single reversible tap (one tap on **Feed** logs, Undo for 5 s) as that alternative, so the card's Feed button has no confirm step; the `?feed=1` shortcut keeps one (see above).
- Undo of a one-tap feeding deletes that entry only; it is offered only to members with `pets.deleteLogs`.
- Duplicate names are rejected in the add dialog against the loaded list (case-insensitive); IDs carry a random suffix, so two clients adding the same name at once still create two documents.
- Reduced motion (system or in-app) shows end states instantly: no blink, idle sway, ring transition, paw burst, card stagger, count ticker or photo morph.
