# Pets and Care Logs

## Purpose and Scope

This module owns pet profiles, pet photos and species art, the living pet cards, pet detail pages, and feeding, medication, and general care histories. Medication countdowns also produce server-side reminders: when a medication's next due day arrives (household time zone), hourly upkeep writes a `pets` notification, using the same countdown rule as the UI; see [Notifications](../notifications/README.md).

## User-Facing Capabilities

- `/pets` lists pets as living cards (H09). Cards enter with the shared `Stagger` (at most 30 ms apart, 400 ms in all; instant under reduced motion). The page header holds the title and, for members who may edit profiles, the **Add Pet** action in the same row (as Shopping and Maintenance). With no pets, the shared `EmptyState` (`kind="pets"`) invites the member to add the first pet (the invitation and button only appear with `pets.editProfiles`).

Chip text is 12 px, 14 px on touch screens (read from across the room on the wall tablet). The card shows two; more fold into a "+N" chip whose tooltip and screen-reader text name the rest (never "+1": a third chip is shown instead). The detail summary shows up to four and the Medication tab shows all;

If someone fed the pet in the last 90 minutes, or today's planned feedings are all logged, the tap asks first: "Feed Rex again?" with "Sam fed Rex 12 min ago." (Feed again / Cancel). The button's tooltip names the global shortcut ("Log a feeding from anywhere  F" on hover or keyboard focus, with `aria-keyshortcuts="F"`) through the shared `ShortcutHint` (`src/components/ui/shortcut-hint.tsx`, keys from `SHORTCUTS` in `src/lib/dashboard/shortcuts.ts`). Success plays `playFeedback('complete')`, or `'celebrate'` when the feeding completes the day (by the operation's committed count), shows a short paw-print burst (skipped under reduced motion) and a toast with **Undo** (10 s) for everyone who may log; a spinner shows while saving, and a failure toast offers **Retry**.

A ring over the photo fills while holding (it appears after 150 ms, so a quick tap never flashes it), then paw prints burst from the photo. Releasing early, pointer cancel (for example a scroll), moving off the photo and Escape cancel without writing; a held or cancelled press never opens the pet page, while a quick tap still does. One hold logs one feeding: holding past the end does nothing more, and a press while a feeding is saving is ignored. Mouse, pen and touch all work (the link under the photo is not draggable and has no touch callout). Under reduced motion the hold still works, with a still ring and no burst. Opening a pet morphs the card photo into the pet page hero (H12): on a plain click the card photo takes the shared name `pet-<id>-photo` (`petPhotoMorphName`, via `sharedId`) only for that navigation (so the theme reveal never captures it), and `useMorphNavigation` runs the View Transition; the hero on `/pets/[petId]`, its in-page loading skeleton and the route skeleton (`PetPhotoSkeleton`, from the route's `petId`) carry the same name. Modified clicks (new tab), clicks swallowed by hold to feed and every click under reduced motion are plain navigations. Hovering (mouse, `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) or keyboard-focusing the pet link for 400 ms shows the shared `PeekCard` (H26) with the type and usual portion, the schedule, the latest feeding and every current medication; it is `aria-hidden`, so the link name is unchanged. The home-screen shortcut **Log feeding** opens `/pets?feed=1`. This is the only feeding path with a confirm step: a launcher shortcut is not a tap on a pet, so it must not log silently. Once both the pet list and the member's permissions (auth and household membership) have loaded (`quickFeedDecision`), members with `pets.addLogs` get a **Log a feeding** dialog: straight to the confirm step ("Log 1 can of dry food for Pixel?" with "Fed 12 min ago by Sam · 1 of 2 today", **Log feeding** or Cancel) with one pet, a pet list first with several, each row showing that fed status (PE-19; "Checking last feeding…" while loading, "Last feeding unavailable" on a failed read). It logs through the same operation, toast and Undo; when the operation asks, "Sam fed Pixel 12 min ago. Feed again?" replaces the step in place (Feed again / Cancel), and the dialog stays open on a failure. - `/pets/[petId]` shows the photo or species art (the morph target), the same feeding summary, medication chips and feed button, the schedule and usual portion, and tabs for Feeding, Medication and Care. `?tab=feeding|medication|care` selects a tab. Each tab lists history first; the full form opens behind **Log with details**, **Log a dose** or **Log care**. History entries show who logged them (member avatar and name) and properly pluralized amounts ("1 cup", "1.5 cups").
- The feeding form defaults to the planned portion and unit and the last food type, accepts any amount above 0 up to 50 (`step="any"`, so a parsed fraction such as "1/3 cup" → 0.33 is a valid default; the pet form and the parser use the same bounds via `isValidPortion`/`PORTION_MAX`), falls back to Dry when the last entry's food type is not a known value, and hides the date and time fields while "Use current time" is checked. Saving goes through the shared feed operation: with the current time (or a chosen time within a minute of now) it is a live feeding with the "Feed again?" check; a chosen earlier time is a backdated entry (`backdated: true`, `date` = the chosen time, no check); a time more than a minute ahead is refused inline ("Pick a time that has already passed."). The medication form has an optional **Next dose due** date that drives the countdown.
- Rules added with these dialogs never block editing an older pet: in the edit dialog a value the member did not change (the locked name, a long schedule, feedings per day, portion) is exempt from the length and range checks; a changed value must pass them. Legacy species values stay selectable.
- The edit dialog resets on every opening, so a discarded draft or replacement photo never carries into the next save. Removing a photo in either dialog is a draft change; it does not submit the form (the shared uploader's untyped Remove button is wrapped so its click cannot submit).
- Add and edit dialogs share one form: photo, name, type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Small mammal, Reptile, Other; a legacy stored value stays selectable), food schedule, and optional feedings per day and usual portion (amount and unit). Field problems are shown inline under the field with `FieldError` (`aria-invalid`, focus moves to the first problem): missing or too long name, a name already used in the household (case-insensitive), missing schedule, feedings per day outside 1-12, portion outside (0, 50]. Toasts are only for outcomes; failures use `toastError`, so messages come from `friendlyError`.
- Deleting a pet or a log asks for confirmation; the pet alert names the pet. A feeding's delete button shows for members with `pets.deleteLogs`, and to its author for 10 minutes after logging (PE-2); deleting a feeding goes through `undoFeed`, so the feeding state stays right.
- Pet lists, pet documents and all three log histories are live (`onSnapshot`): changes by other members or devices appear without a refresh.

Species avatars (`src/components/pets/species-avatar.tsx`) are inline SVG for dog, cat, bird, fish, small mammal, reptile and other (a paw print), drawn in the EmptyState style with token classes only (`fill-primary/10` backdrop, `fill-muted` shapes outlined in `currentColor`, `fill-foreground` eyes, one `primary` accent), so every preset recolors them. Eyes blink (140 ms every 7 s, staggered between cards), and the figure idles: a 24 s ambient loop that sways and bobs it over the still backdrop by 1.5 px and 1.5 degrees at the default intensity (staggered between cards). The blink depth and the sway amplitude scale with the preset's single `--ambient-intensity` token (via `--ambient-scale`, 1 at 6%), shared with the aurora, shell tint and weather, so turning it to 0 holds the pet still (guardrail G5). Both are CSS-only animations that pause while the tab is hidden (`html[data-visibility="hidden"]`) and are removed under both `prefers-reduced-motion` and `html[data-motion="reduce"]`. The art stays SVG/CSS rather than the spec's Lottie (approved deviation: no new dependencies). The type picks the art; for "Other" the photo hint (`dataAiHint`, e.g. "rabbit") is used. Motion lives in `src/components/pets/pets.module.css`: the ring fill is a 500 ms data-change transition and the paw burst is 900 ms of opacity/transform in an absolutely positioned, `aria-hidden`, pointer-transparent layer, so nothing shifts or blocks input.

## Architecture and Data Flow

The pet list subscribes to the household pet collection; each card subscribes to its pet's newest 20 feeding logs for the feeding status. The medication countdowns (card, detail summary and Medication tab) does not use a bounded history window: `useMedicationCountdownLogs` subscribes to every medication entry with a `nextDueDate` and to every entry from the oldest pending medication's latest dated dose onward (`pendingWindowStart`), so a monthly dose stays visible however many daily doses follow, and a later dose of the same medication still supersedes it. Both queries use single-field indexes.

Log dates exist in two stored shapes: Firestore timestamps (feedings written by this app) and ISO strings (medication and care entries, and legacy feedings). Firestore orders values of different types separately, so one `orderBy('date')` with a limit could hide every entry of one shape (for example, new timestamp feedings behind 20 legacy string feedings). Every history and countdown query therefore runs once per shape (`date >= <min timestamp>` and `date >= ''`, each ordered and limited the same way), and `newestFirst` merges them chronologically before applying the limit. No migration is needed; documents keep their stored shape. The countdown's "since" queries use the same pair (the string bound has a one-day margin for date-only strings). Timestamp dates on medication or care entries are converted to ISO strings on read. The detail route subscribes to one pet document and mounts a live client per log collection (newest 100 entries each). Photo uploads happen before the pet document is written with the resulting download URL.

The feeding plan comes from the structured `feedingsPerDay`, `portionAmount` and `portionUnit` fields when set, otherwise from parsing the free-text schedule ("1.5 cups twice daily" → 2 × 1.5 cups; "One can morning and evening" → 2 × 1 can; ".5 cups" → 0.5 cups, never a number read from inside another one; amounts are rounded to two decimals and ignored above 50). Explicitly non-daily or irregular schedules ("twice a week", "every other day", "every 3 days", "as needed", "free feed", "Mondays") get no daily target, so the ring never claims a daily goal the member did not enter; "every 8 hours" counts as 3 a day. A stored structured portion outside the accepted bounds is ignored for the plan, otherwise one cup with no daily target (the ring then fills on the first feeding). "Today" is the viewer's local calendar day.

## Data Model and Persistence

- Pet: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}`.
- Feeding logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-logs/{logId}`.
- Feeding state: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/feeding-state/current` (see below).
- Medication logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/medication-logs/{logId}`.
- Care logs: `households/{householdId}/pets/{petId}/care-logs/{logId}`.
- Photos: `households/{householdId}/pets/{uuid}.{extension}`.

Pet IDs are the slugified name plus a random suffix. New log entries use Firestore auto IDs; older entries keep their numeric IDs. Feeding dates are written as Firestore timestamps (server time for live feedings, PE-5) and reads accept timestamp or ISO values; medication and care dates are ISO strings. A server time that is still pending in a local snapshot reads as its local estimate.

Additive optional fields (all absent on older documents, which read as before):

- Pet: `feedingsPerDay` (integer 1-12), `portionAmount` (number), `portionUnit` (`Cups`, `Cans`, `Scoops`, `Other`). Clearing them in the edit dialog removes the fields. `type` may now also be `Bird`, `Fish`, `Small mammal` or `Reptile`.
- Feeding, medication and care logs: `loggedByUid` and `loggedByName` (the member's first name at the time of logging).
- Feeding logs written by the shared feed operation: `loggedAt` (server time of the write), `repeat` (`none`; `confirmed` after "Feed again?"; `offline` when queued offline), `clockSource: 'device'` (offline only: `date` is that device's clock) and `backdated: true` (a chosen past time from the detailed form).
- Medication logs: `nextDueDate` (`yyyy-MM-dd`, local calendar date).

A pet without a photo is still written with the legacy placeholder URL `https://placehold.co/300x300.png` so older clients keep rendering it; current readers treat that URL and an empty value as "no photo".

### Shared feed operation (PE-23, canonical for PE-1; PE-5; PE-2)

Every feeding entry point (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` picker, the command palette's quick add `logFeeding`, and the feeding-log form, live and backdated) calls `feedPet` in `src/lib/pets/feed-operation.ts`; there is no other feeding writer (`addFeedingLog` was removed and `pet-log-api.ts` refuses the `feeding-logs` kind). The dashboard's one-tap **Fed** (HP-33a: `PetFedButton` in `src/components/dashboard/one-tap.tsx`, on a hungry pet's house chip and Pets tile row) uses it too, through `useFeedAction` and `FeedAgainDialog`, with the pet's planned portion and the latest feeding's food type from the dashboard's loaded history.

`feeding-state/current` holds `lastLogId`, `lastFedAt` (timestamp), `lastFedByUid`, `lastFedByName`, `day` (household-zone `yyyy-MM-dd`, the day `countOnDay` counts), `countOnDay`, and `prev` (the same six fields before the latest feed, for Undo; null after an Undo or for the first feed).

- **Online**: one transaction reads the pet (its plan's feedings per day), the state and the state's latest log (a latest feed whose log was deleted offline is not a feed), and, on every attempt, the surviving feedings from the server since the household day's start or 90 minutes ago, whichever is earlier (`dayFeedings` from `feedReadStart`: both date shapes, at most 100 each, newest first), so a feeding just before household midnight (for example an offline feed that synced at 23:55) still counts as recent at 00:05 even when the state document is older. Today's count is counted from today's logs among them (`countOnHouseholdDay`; the minutes before midnight never add to it), never taken from the stored count or the caller's loaded logs, so a feed queued offline that has synced is counted (online, offline, online gives 3), and a feeding deleted offline or while "Feed again?" was open is not; any feed or Undo by someone else writes the state, so the transaction retries with a fresh read. Then it runs `feedConfirmDecision`: when the last known feed is under 90 minutes old (`FEED_REPEAT_WINDOW_MS`; a time in the future counts as recent) or today's count `>= perDay`, it returns `needsConfirm` with who fed and when, and nothing is written; the UI asks "Sam fed Rex 12 min ago. Feed again?" ("You fed…" for your own feed; "Rex has had all 2 feedings today." for a complete day) and calls again with `confirm`. Otherwise it creates the log (`date` and `loggedAt` = `serverTimestamp()`, `loggedByUid`/`loggedByName` of the signed-in member, `repeat` `none` or `confirmed`) and sets the state in the same commit, so two people feeding at once cannot both pass the check.
- **Offline** (the browser reports no network; transactions cannot run): the log alone is queued in a batch with `repeat: 'offline'`, `clockSource: 'device'` and `date` = this device's clock; the check only uses what the device has (its cached state and loaded logs), and the toast says "Saved on this device. Couldn't check whether someone else fed Rex." only once the write is queued. The state is not written offline (a blind queued write could replace a newer feed by someone else); the next online feed or Undo updates it, and every reader combines the state with the logs it has (the newer wins). A transaction that fails online (for example a dropped connection) shows a failure toast with Retry; nothing is claimed as saved.
- **Backdated** (the detailed form with a chosen earlier time): `backdated: true`, `date` = the chosen time, no repeat check; it becomes the state's latest feed only when it is newer than every known feed, otherwise it only adds to the count of its own household day (a different day than `day` leaves the count alone).
- **Undo** (`undoFeed`, from the 10 s toast and from the history's delete): a transaction deletes the log; when `lastLogId` is that log it restores `prev` (only if `prev`'s log still exists, read in the same transaction; otherwise the latest feed is cleared and readers fall back to their loaded logs) and sets `countOnDay` to today's surviving feedings (read from the server in the transaction, without the deleted log; this also repairs a count left by an earlier offline delete); when a newer feed came since, only the count changes, and if the deleted log is the one `prev` points at, `prev` is cleared too (feed A, feed B, delete A, delete B never brings A back). It never writes an old whole-document snapshot over newer data. Offline only the log delete is queued; the next online feed or Undo recounts the day from the logs. Undoing the record does not undo the meal (audit 17.2, WT-20): the next feed still runs the last-fed check against the restored state.
- **No state yet** (pets fed before this release, until the backfill runs): readers treat it as "no recent feed known" and fall back to the newest logs they have loaded (`knownFromLogs`: the card's newest 20, or the palette's newest entry per date shape); the first online feed creates the state (its count comes from today's logs, as always online); the palette reads the newest 20 feedings per date shape, as the cards do.
- "Today" for the count is the household day (`household.timeZone`, default America/Chicago), computed with `localDay` from `functions/src/shared/calendar.ts` (`householdDay`); no shared `src/lib/household-day.ts` exists on main yet, so switch to it when the dashboard data PR adds one. The day is estimated with this device's clock at commit, so a feed within seconds of midnight on a badly skewed device can land on the neighbouring day. The ring and "today" on the cards still use the viewer's local day, and a feeding up to 12 h in the future (`FEEDING_CLOCK_SKEW_MS`, a slow device clock reading server time) counts as just now instead of being hidden; further ahead it is ignored.
- `FEED_UNDO_MS` (10 000 ms) is local to the Pets module: main has no shared Undo duration yet.

Migration: `node scripts/ops/backfill-feeding-state.mjs --project <id>` (dry run, counts only; `--apply` creates; `--firebase-tools-login` as the other ops scripts) creates `feeding-state/current` for every pet that has feedings and no state, from its newest 20 feedings per date shape (`feedingStateFromLogs` in `scripts/ops/feeding-state.mjs`: the newest becomes the latest feed, `countOnDay` counts that log's household day). Create-only and resumable: an existing state is never touched, and one created by a client meanwhile wins. Run it after rules R1 are deployed.

## Authentication, Roles, and Security

Pages require an authenticated current user and scope all paths with that user's `householdId`. Firestore and Storage rules treat `households/{householdId}/members/{uid}` as the only membership authority: the caller needs an approved member record (role `owner`, `admin`, `member`, `child`, or `guest` with `status` `active`), and `households.memberEmails` or user-profile fields never grant access. Pending `newuser` members are blocked.

Firestore rules enforce each pet permission (role preset plus per-member overrides; owners hold every permission): `pets.view` to read pets and logs, `pets.editProfiles` to create, update, or delete pet documents, `pets.addLogs` to create or update feeding, medication, and care logs, and `pets.deleteLogs` to delete logs. The UI now mirrors these with the effective `permissions` from auth context: Add Pet, the empty-state action and the pet actions menu need `pets.editProfiles`; feed buttons and log forms need `pets.addLogs`; log delete buttons and the feeding Undo need `pets.deleteLogs`. Hiding controls is a convenience only; the rules remain the enforcement point.

Log shapes (QA-8): feeding logs may only set `date` (timestamp or string), `cups` (number 0 to 1000), `foodType`, `foodAmountType`, `comments` (up to 2000), `ampm` (`AM`/`PM`), `loggedByUid` and `loggedByName`; medication logs `date`, `medication`, `dosage` (up to 200 each), `notes` (up to 2000), `nextDueDate`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName`; care logs `date`, `activity` (up to 200), `notes`, `loggedByUid`, `loggedByName`. On create, `loggedByUid`, when present, must be the writer's uid and `loggedByName` is only accepted with it; both stay optional because clients cached from before 2026-11-06 write neither. On update both are immutable (no change, no removal), so an old client's "count + 1" overwrite of a newer signed entry is refused instead of erasing who logged it; overwriting an unsigned legacy entry still works.

Feeding rules, stage R1 (this release, compatible with every shipped client): feeding logs also allow `loggedAt` (a timestamp equal to `request.time` on create, immutable afterwards), `repeat` (`none`/`confirmed`/`offline`), `clockSource` (`device`/`server`) and `backdated` (bool). A feeding may be deleted with `pets.deleteLogs`, or by its author (`loggedByUid`, with `pets.addLogs`) until 10 minutes after `loggedAt` (PE-2), so Undo works for members and children; older entries without `loggedAt` need `pets.deleteLogs`. `feeding-state/{id}` allows only `current`: read with `pets.view`; no delete; every write must have the six fields with valid types (`countOnDay` an integer 0-1000, `day` `yyyy-MM-dd` or null, `prev` null or the same six fields). A create, or an update that changes `lastLogId`, needs `pets.addLogs`, `lastFedByUid == request.auth.uid`, a log at `lastLogId` that exists after the write (`existsAfter`) and is signed by the writer, and `prev` equal to the replaced state's six fields exactly (null only on create, when there was no state), so a member cannot plant a fabricated "previous feeder" for a later Undo to restore. With `pets.addLogs` or `pets.deleteLogs` an update may also change only `day`/`countOnDay`; clear `prev` (with the count) when the log `prev` points at is gone after the write; or, when the current `lastLogId` log is gone after the write (Undo), restore exactly the stored `prev` while its log still exists, or clear the latest feed only when there is no surviving `prev` (null never discards a feed that still exists).

Stage R2 (the next release, after one release window with R1 and the backfill; not deployed yet): require on live feeding-log creates `date == request.time` unless `backdated == true` (then `date < request.time`) or `repeat == 'offline'` (then `clockSource == 'device'`); require every non-offline feeding create to be paired with a `feeding-state/current` write in the same commit (`getAfter(state).lastLogId == logId`, or an unchanged latest with a count change for older backdated entries); and refuse a live create inside 90 minutes of the state's `lastFedAt` or on a complete day unless `repeat == 'confirmed'`. Rolling back R2 means redeploying R1; watch denied writes from old clients during the window.

Pet Storage rules require `pets.view` to read and `pets.editProfiles` to upload, replace, or delete photos. Uploads must be 10 MB or smaller with content type `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/webp`, `image/gif`, `image/heic`, `image/heif`, or `image/avif`.

## Integrations and Background Processing

Firebase Storage holds pet photos. The hourly upkeep Cloud Function (`functions/src/reminders.ts` with `functions/src/shared/medication-reminders.ts`) reads pets and their medication logs and writes medication reminders (`sourceType` `pet_medication`, due today or overdue, resolved once a newer dose is logged, and reopened if that dose is deleted); its pushes show the pet's photo. No AI flow or external API is involved. Sound and haptics go through the shared opt-in `playFeedback`.

## Cross-Module Dependencies

The homepage AI Overview may include pet count and a seven-day care-log count for callers with `pets.view`. It does not infer health, neglect, or missed care and does not send raw pet histories to the model.

- [Identity and Profile](../identity-profile/README.md) and [Household Governance](../household-governance/README.md) supply user, household, role and effective permission context.
- [Firebase Platform](../firebase-platform/README.md) owns Firestore/Storage initialization and security rules.
- [Application Shell and Dashboard](../application-shell/README.md) reads pets and feedings read-only for the dashboard (both date shapes, judged with `feedingPlan`/`feedingStatus`, so "fed" there means today's target is met, as on the pet card) logs one-tap **Fed** on hungry pets through `useFeedAction` (the same operation, "Feed again?" check and 10 s Undo), and logs quick-add feedings through the shared feed operation (`logFeeding` → `feedPet`, with its "Feed again?" check in the palette preview and a 10 s Undo) with the pet's usual portion unless an amount is given and the latest feeding's food type (`knownFoodType`, Dry without history), as the Feed button does (amounts parsed with `parsePortion`; gated by `pets.addLogs`). It also exposes pet navigation and the **Log feeding** home-screen shortcut (`/pets`), and provides the design foundation used here (`EmptyState`, `MemberAvatar`, `FieldError`, `toastError`/`friendlyError`, `playFeedback`, `useReducedMotion`, tokens, `glow-card`).
- [Notifications](../notifications/README.md) receives medication reminders (category `pets`) from hourly upkeep, deep-linking to `/pets/<petId>?tab=medication`. Changing the countdown rule in `src/lib/pets/care.ts` (`currentMedications`) must be mirrored in `functions/src/shared/medication-reminders.ts`.
- [Chores](../chores/README.md) owns the household calendar, which reads every medication entry read-only and shows logged doses plus upcoming doses computed with `currentMedications` (no separate rule).

## Invariants and Failure Behavior

- Log entries can be created and deleted but not edited.
- Feeding, medication, and care history deletions share a target-specific confirmation dialog with cancel and confirm paths.
- Deleting a pet document does not recursively delete nested logs or Storage photos.
- Replacing or removing a photo does not delete the previous Storage object.
- A missing pet shows an `EmptyState` with a link back to all pets; a load failure shows a friendly toast and a Try again action.
- A failed log subscription is never shown as an empty history. The card and the detail page share `PetCareSummary`: a feeding load error shows "Feeding status is unavailable right now" (no ring, no "No feedings logged yet"), and a medication load error shows "Next medication dose is unavailable right now" instead of hiding the chip. The feed button stays available, but with an unknown status it never claims "all fed for today". The Medication tab shows "Next dose is unavailable right now", and each history tab shows its own unavailable message. A unit test checks that every consumer of `usePetLogs` and `useMedicationCountdownLogs` reads `error`.
- Quick feedings (Feed button, hold to feed, the `?feed=1` confirm) never log before the pet's feeding history has loaded (`quickFeedFood`): until then the Feed button and **Log feeding** are disabled (the confirm shows "Checking Pixel's last feeding…") and a hold does not start, so a fast tap can never record a default Dry entry for a pet that eats wet food. Once loaded they log the latest feeding's food type (Dry with no history). If the history cannot be read, feeding stays available as Dry and says so ("…of dry food logged (last feeding unavailable)" in the toast, and a note in the confirm); unavailable history is never treated as empty.
- H09 accessible alternative (owner decision, wave 4): the audit's acceptance asks hold to feed for "a tap-plus-confirm alternative". The owner reads the single reversible tap (one tap on **Feed** logs, Undo for 10 s) as that alternative, so the card's Feed button has no confirm step of its own; it asks only when the shared operation's last-fed check does (fed under 90 minutes ago, or today complete), and the `?feed=1` shortcut keeps its confirm (see above).
- Undo of a feeding deletes that entry only (and keeps the feeding state honest, see above); it is offered for 10 s to everyone who may log, and the rules accept it from the author for 10 minutes.
- Duplicate names are rejected in the add dialog against the loaded list (case-insensitive); IDs carry a random suffix, so two clients adding the same name at once still create two documents.
- Reduced motion (system or in-app) shows end states instantly: no blink, idle sway, ring transition, paw burst, card stagger, count ticker or photo morph.

## When This Document Must Be Updated

Update this README when pet/log fields or paths, photo lifecycle, log ID strategy, role enforcement or permission-based visibility, the feeding plan or status rules, species art, reminder generation, routes, or delete cleanup behavior changes.

Feeding rules, stage R1 (this release, compatible with every shipped client): feeding logs also allow `loggedAt` (a timestamp equal to `request.time` on create, immutable afterwards), `repeat` (`none`/`confirmed`/`offline`), `clockSource` (`device`/`server`) and `backdated` (bool). A feeding may be deleted with `pets.deleteLogs`, or by its author (`loggedByUid`, with `pets.addLogs`) until 10 minutes after `loggedAt` (PE-2), so Undo works for members and children; older entries without `loggedAt` need `pets.deleteLogs`. `feeding-state/{id}` allows only `current`: read with `pets.view`; no delete; every write must have the six fields with valid types (`countOnDay` an integer 0-1000, `day` `yyyy-MM-dd` or null, `prev` null or the same six fields). A create, or an update that changes `lastLogId`, needs `pets.addLogs`, `lastFedByUid == request.auth.uid`, a log at `lastLogId` that exists after the write (`existsAfter`) and is signed by the writer, and `prev` equal to the replaced state's six fields exactly (null only on create, when there was no state), so a member cannot plant a fabricated "previous feeder" for a later Undo to restore. With `pets.addLogs` or `pets.deleteLogs` an update may also change only `day`/`countOnDay`; clear `prev` (with the count) when the log `prev` points at is gone after the write; or, when the current `lastLogId` log is gone after the write (Undo), restore exactly the stored `prev` while its log still exists, or clear the latest feed only when there is no surviving `prev` (null never discards a feed that still exists).

`feeding-state/current` holds `lastLogId`, `lastFedAt` (timestamp), `lastFedByUid`, `lastFedByName`, `day` (household-zone `yyyy-MM-dd`, the day `countOnDay` counts), `countOnDay`, and `prev` (the same six fields before the latest feed, for Undo; null after an Undo or for the first feed).

These rules ship ahead of the shared feed operation that writes the new fields; clients without it are unaffected.
